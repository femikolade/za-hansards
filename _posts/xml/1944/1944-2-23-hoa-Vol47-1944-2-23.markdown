I passed the bus, and I stopped, and I thought I should ask whether I could give some assistance. They did not even bother to greet me. Even then I said: &#x201C;Friend, is there anything I can do for you?&#x201D; To this day I have not had a reply. That I look upon as discrimination. Those people knew that only a few weeks ago the majority of the voters had elected me to Parliament, they knew where I was going, but they discriminated against me and would not even talk to me.</p>
