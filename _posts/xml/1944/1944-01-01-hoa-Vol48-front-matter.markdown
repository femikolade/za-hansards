<p>From the 21st April the Hon. J. H. Hofmeyr acted as Prime Minister and Minister of External Affairs and the Hon. F. C. Sturrock as Minister of Defence during the absence from the Union of Field-Marshal the Rt. Hon. J. C. Smuts.</p>

<p>From 11th April, 1944, the following designations took effect:</p>

<p>Minister of Railways and Harbours: MINISTER OF TRANSPORT.</p>
