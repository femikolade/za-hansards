<heading>Importation of Tung Oil</heading>

<p>The MINISTER OF ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT replied to Question No. I by Mr. Raubenheimer standing over from 14th April:</p>

<question by="#raubenheimer" to="#minister_of_economic_development">

<heading>Question:</heading>

<ol>

<li>(1) What quantities of tung oil were imported during each of the last three years;</li>

<li><span class="col_6299-6300" refersTo="page_0577"/>(2) from which countries was such oil imported and what quantities from each country;</li>

<li>(3) what was the value of the oil per 2,000 lbs. ton imported each year;</li>

<li>(4) what quantities were produced by mills in the Union during each of the last three years and what was the value of such quantities;</li>

<li>(5) what is the estimated amount needed for the requirements of the Union;</li>

<li>(6) what were the London spot prices per ton in August, 1942, August, 1943, and March, 1944;</li>

<li>(7) whether the Government will undertake not to allow tung oil to be imported until the Union crops of nuts have been disposed of; and</li>

<li>(8) whether the Government intends fixing a fair price or allowing growers to ship to England or the United States of America and providing shipping priority.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_economic_development" to="#raubenheimer">

<heading>Reply:</heading>

<ol>

<li>(1) 1941: 62&#x00BE; short tons; 1942: 32 short tons; 1943: 30 short tons.</li>

<li>(2) 23&#x00BD; short tons from the United States of America; 48&#x00BE; short tons from Hongkong; 52&#x00BD; short tons from Nyasaland;</li>

<li>(3) 1941: approximately &#x00A3;14; 1942: approximately &#x00A3;14; 1943: approximately &#x00A3;9;</li>

<li>(4) 1941: 6 short tons, total value &#x00A3;150; 1942: 14&#x00BE; short tons, total value &#x00A3;225 15s.; 1943: 31&#x00BE; short tons, total value &#x00A3;285 17s. 6d.;</li>

<li>(5) Approximately 60 short tons per annum;</li>

<li>(6) It is regretted that this information is not available;</li>

<li>(7) such an undertaking cannot be given, but I may state that it is customary to allow importation only after an estimate has been made of what is locally available;</li>

<li>(8) the fixation of the price of tung oil produced in the Union is receiving consideration. The export of tung oil cannot be allowed as local supplies are insufficient to meet the Union&#x2019;s essential requirements.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
