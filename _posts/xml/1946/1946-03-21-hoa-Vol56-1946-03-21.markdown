<p><i>Limitation on Proceedings in Committee of Supply.</i></p>

<speech by="#prime_minister">

<from>*The <person refersTo="hansard_za">PRIME MINISTER</person>:</from>

<p>I move&#x2014;</p>

<block name="quote">That proceedings in Committee of Supply on the Estimates of Expenditure to be defrayed from the Consolidated Revenue and Railway and Harbour Funds for the year ending 31st March, 1947, be limited to 125 hours including the time already occupied.</block>

<p>For the purpose of this resolution&#x2014;</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Reference of Estimates to Committee of Supply.&#x2014;All Estimates of Expenditure for the financial year 1946-&#x2019;47 which may be laid upon the Table and recommended by the Governor-General, shall stand referred to the Committee.</li>

<li>(2) Conclusion of proceedings.&#x2014;At the conclusion of the period of hours allotted, any amendments (other than amendments proposed by a Minister) which have been moved but not disposed of shall drop. The Chairman shall thereupon proceed to put forthwith, without debate, any amendments which have been moved or may be moved by a Minister and thereafter only such further amendments as may be moved by a Minister and such questions, including votes, items or heads, as amended or as printed, as may be necessary to dispose of the Estimates under consideration.<br/>The Committee shall then proceed to consider any Estimates of Expenditure which have been referred to it but have not been considered, and the Chairman shall forthwith put the votes, items or heads in such Estimates without amendment (unless amendments are moved by a Minister) and without debate.</li>

<li>(3) Expedition of Report Stage.&#x2014;The Committee shall have leave to bring up its report or reports forthwith, instead of on a future day, and such report or reports shall be considered forthwith without amendment or debate.</li>

<li>(4) Time for adjournment of House.&#x2014; When business is interrupted at the conclusion of the period of hours allotted the application of Sessional Orders fixing the time for the adjournment of the House shall be postponed until the proceedings on the business interrupted have been completed.</li>

<li>(5) Dilatory motions.&#x2014;At no time while the House is in Committee shall the Chairman receive a motion that the Chairman report progress or do leave the Chair, or a motion to postpone a vote, item or head unless moved by&#x2019; a Minister and the Question on such motion shall be put forthwith without debate.</li></ol>

<p>I understand that this motion is the outcome of discussions which have taken place between the various parties in the House and that it expresses the general feeling of the parties.</p>

</speech>

To use another metaphor, the speech of the Prime Minister as we heard it, was an attempt such as we had in the days of the old Greeks when they were caught between two dangers, when they wanted to avoid a danger on the one side as well as another danger on the other side, and they sailed most carefully between Scylla and Charybdis. <p>There are certain outstanding facts, I think, in connection with the international situation which we dare not ignore. They practically and actually determine the whole matter.
