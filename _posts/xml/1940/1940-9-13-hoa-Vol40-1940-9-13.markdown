<from>Mr. HEMMING</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Railways and Harbours:</p>

<p>Whether, in view of the loss of life and injuries sustained as a result of the recent accident on the Umtata&#x2014;Amabele branch line, it is intended to hold an enquiry into the causes thereof; if so, (a) who will constitute the board of enquiry, (b) what will be the scope of such enquiry, and (c) whether he is prepared (i) to arrange for such enquiry to be held in public, and (ii) to appoint a representative of the Native Affairs Department as a member of such board of enquiry.</p>

<p>It has been recommended to His Excellency the Governor-General that a board of enquiry be appointed in terms of section 68 of Act No. 22 of 1916 to make a public enquiry into the cause of the regrettable accident to a native train which occurred at Amabele on the 6th September, 1940.</p>

</answer>

<li>(2) whether he will take steps to ensure that they supply such information before being permitted to depart from the Union; and</li>

<li><span class="col_1609-1610" refersTo="page_0878"/>(3) whether he will take all necessary measures to prevent emigrants taking capital out of the country by means of precious metals, stones, jewellery or other valuables.</li>

<from>The MINISTER OF THE INTERIOR:</from>

<li>(1)

<ol>

<li>(a) and</li>

<li>(b) Yes, in the case of all Union nationals between the ages of 16 and 60.</li>

The reference to &#x201C;bills&#x201D; in this connection is not understood.</li></ol></li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_justice" to="#van_nierop">

<from>The MINISTER OF JUSTICE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(2) Yes.</li>

<li>(3) While no attacks are anticipated every effort is made to afford general police protection. <debateSection name="#customs_agreement_between_the_union_of_s_a_and_great_britain_and_northern_ireland">

<heading>CUSTOMS AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE UNION OF S.A. AND GREAT BRITAIN AND NORTHERN IRELAND.</heading>

<speech by="#minister_of_commerce_and_industries">

<from>&#x2020;THE <person refersTo="hansard_za">MINISTER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRIES</person>:</from>

<p>I move&#x2014;</p>

<block name="quote">That this House, in terms of section 10 (1) of the Customs Tariff Amendment Act, 1939, and section 8 of the Customs Tariff and Excise Duties Amendment Act, 1925, approves of the agreement concluded between the Governments of the Union of South Africa and the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, to effect a temporary amendment of the provisions of Schedule D of Part I of the First Schedule to the Ottawa Agreements Act, 1933, copies whereof were laid upon the Table on the 12th September, 1940.</block>

<p>This motion and the following one are formal motions for the House to adopt certain agreements which have been entered into between ourselves, in the one case, with the United Kingdom and in the other case with Canada. Under the Ottawa agreements the United Kingdom agreed to maintain a duty of 10 per cent. on all foreign imports, of white fish and hake. Soon after the war broke out they asked us if we would agree to the suspension of that duty on foreign imports in order that they could make a gesture to Iceland and Scandinavia regarding their imports of fish. I may say that it does not affect South Africa at all. The imports into the United Kingdom of fish from the Union in 1937 were &#x00A3;82, in 1938 &#x00A3;18 and in 1939 &#x00A3;130. Of course it affects other Dominions more than it does us, and for that reason we agreed that for twelve months, from the date of the agreement, we would agree to a suspension of this duty.</p>

</speech>

<from>Mr. <person refersTo="hansard_za">FRIEND</person>:</from>

<p>Motion put and agreed to.</p>

<heading><span class="col_1701-1702" refersTo="page_0924"/>CUSTOMS AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE UNION OF S.A. AND CANADA.</heading>

<speech by="#minister_of_commerce_and_industries">

<from>&#x2020;The <person refersTo="hansard_za">MINISTER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRIES</person>:</from>

<p>I move&#x2014;</p>

<block name="quote">That this House, in terms of section 10 (1) of the Customs Tariff Amendment Act, 1939, and section 8 of the Customs Tariff and Excise Duties Amendment Act, 1925, approves of the agreement concluded between the Governments of the Union of South Africa and Canada, to effect a temporary amendment of the provisions of Article IV of Part II of the First Schedule to the Ottawa Agreements Act, 1933, copies whereof were laid upon the Table on the 12th September, 1940.</block>

<p>This is the other motion to which I referred. Under the Ottawa agreements Canada has to maintain a duty of 10 cents a lb. on wool as against foreign nations, and at the beginning of this year Canada had certain difficulties in getting wool and asked us to suspend the duty for four months, that is for January, February, March and April. We agreed to do that and it has had no effect whatever on our business, and in addition to that we have been able to meet Canada in this regard.</p>
