<heading>ORAL QUESTION.</heading>

<p>Liberated South African Prisoners of War.</p>

<p>Mr. MARWICK, with leave, asked the Acting Minister of Defence:</p>

<p>Whether he will publish immediately, through every available organ of the Press in South Africa, the names of all South African prisoners of war liberated from the German camp near Bad Orb on Monday last.</p>

<speech by="#minister_of_defence">

<from>The <person refersTo="hansard_za">MINISTER OF DEFENCE</person>:</from>

<p>The Adjutant-General has instructions to make available for publication the names of all ex-prisoners of war immediately they are received.</p>

<p>No information concerning the release of U.D.F. prisoners of war referred to had been received by the Government up till 8.30 this morning.</p>

<p>The information which is being published was received by the Press through their representatives in the United Kingdom.</p>
