<p>Port Elizabeth Municipality: Purchase of Government-owned Land.<b></b></p>

<question by="#hayward" to="#minister_of_lands">

<num>I.</num>

<from>Mr. HAYWARD</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Lands:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether representations have been made by the Port Elizabeth Municipality to either buy or exchange for other land owned by the municipality the Government-owned land known as &#x201C;Drift Sands&#x201D;; if so, how far have negotiations been carried; and</li>

<li>(2) whether, in view of the shortage of housing, he will take immediate steps to either exchange or sell &#x201C;Drift Sands&#x201D; to the municipality at a fair nominal price, on the express condition that it will be immediately utilised for housing schemes and that the reduction in price be passed on to the public.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry" to="#hayward">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE AND FORESTRY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes, but in view of the fact that the post-war needs of Government Departments for portions of the Port Elizabeth Drift Sands Forest Reserve have not yet been decided upon, no final arrangements for the sale of the balance of the Forest Reserve can be made. Moreover, actual sale cannot be effected until Parliament, by resolution, has agreed to the sale, and it is regretted that at this late stage of the Session it is not possible to do so.</li>

<li>(2) Falls away.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
