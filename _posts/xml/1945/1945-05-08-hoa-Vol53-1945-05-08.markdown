<heading>QUESTIONS.</heading>

<p>Bonus for Civil Pensioners.</p>

<question by="#alexander" to="#minister_of_finance">

<num>I.</num>

<from>Mr. ALEXANDER</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Finance:</p>

<p>Whether, in view of the increase in the cost of living shown by the increased cost of living allowances granted to public servants, he will consider the advisability of giving immediate relief also to pensioners.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_finance" to="#alexander">

<from>The MINISTER OF FINANCE:</from>

<p>Provision was made under Section 47 of the Pension Laws Amendment Act, 1943 for the grant of a bonus to civil pensioners who are in necessitous circumstances as a result of conditions arising from the present war.</p>

<p>Consideration has recently been given to extending the scheme of assistance and it has been decided that married European pensioners drawing civil pensions of less than &#x00A3;250 p.a. (previously the limit was &#x00A3;200 p.a.) may be granted a bonus. Corresponding adjustments will be made in the case of Coloured and Native civil pensioners and civil pensioners who are single, or widowers or widows without dependants</p>

</answer>

<question by="#marwick" to="#minister">

<from>&#x2020;Mr. MARWICK:</from>

<p>Arising out of the Minister&#x2019;s reply, can he tell us whether this will dispense with the necessity of appearing before the committee to prove that the applicants are destitute or in poor circumstances.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_finance" to="#marwick">

<from>The MINISTER OF FINANCE:</from>

<p>There is no necessity of appearing before the committee today. Applications are submitted in writing to that committee, but the committee will continue to function.</p>

<ol>

<p>The changes arose primarily from the report of the Public Service Enquiry Commission in which were included certain recommendations concerning cost of living allowances. Over &#x00A3;300 to &#x00A3;600.<br/>

<li>(b) The other principle is that the maximum salary at which the servant is not required to bear a portion of the increased cost of living has been raised from &#x00A3;300 to &#x00A3;350 but the principle has been retained of providing greatest compensation to the lower paid groups in accordance with the declared policy of the Government.</li>

<heading>Question:</heading>

<li>(3) whether any police or natives were killed; if so, how many, respectively;</li>

<li>(4) whether any arrests have been made; if so, how many;</li>

<li>(6) whether police doing duty in such localities are armed; if so, with what weapons;</li>

When the Messenger attempted to take possession of the child, the whole of the sect obstructed him and refused to part with the child. <li>(5) A recurrence of this incident is not anticipated. The natives who attacked the police belong to a religious sect known as &#x201C;Mosheswa&#x201D; and comprise only a small fraction of the natives living in Alexandra Township.</li>

<li>(6) Yes, with batons and revolvers.</li>

<li><span class="col_6911-6912" refersTo="page_0792"/>(7) Yes.</li>

<li>(8) Yes. 213 short of establishment.</li>

<li>(9) Two thousand eight hundred members of the public have been appointed special constables and are known as the Civic Guard. A total of 260 perform duty every night in Johannesburg.
