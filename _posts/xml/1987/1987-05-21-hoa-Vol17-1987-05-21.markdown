<heading>RULING BY MR SPEAKER ON RESUMPTION OF PROCEEDINGS ON BILLS</heading>

<speech by="#speaker">

<from>*Mr <person refersTo="hansard_za">SPEAKER</person>:</from>

<p>With reference to the point of order raised yesterday in connection with the provisions of Rule 40 relating to the resumption of bills in a subsequent session, I give the following ruling.</p>

<block name="quote">The Joint Rules and Orders were introduced by resolutions of the three Houses in the exercise of Parliament&#x2019;s plenary authority to arrange and regulate its business and proceedings by itself. Section 34 (2) (b) of the Constitution affirms Parliament&#x2019;s authority in this respect.</block>

<block name="quote">The validity of Rule 40 (1) is based entirely on the resolutions of the Houses by which it was decreed. The Rule may be suspended by a differing arrangement in the same manner as that in which it may be amended generally, namely, by resolution of each House of Parliament. Indeed, such a suspension of the Rule is nothing but an <i>ad hoc</i> amendment thereof.</block>

<block name="quote">I rule, therefore, that the motion by the Leader of the House is in order.</block>

</speech>
