<heading>Transport Facilities.</heading>

<question by="#van_den_berg" to="#minister_of_railways_and_harbours">

<num>I.</num>

<from>Mr. M. J. VAN DEN BERG</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Railways and Harbours:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) whether the Administration is at present finding it difficult to cope with the heavy demand for transport;</li>

<li>(2) whether many applications for permission to convey persons and goods by private transport have been rejected by the Road Transportation Board; and</li>

<li>(3) whether he intends taking steps to meet the increased demand for the transportation of persons and goods; if so, what steps?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_railways_and_harbours" to="#van_den_berg">

<from>The MINISTER OF RAILWAYS AND HARBOURS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes.</li>

<li>(2) Even in normal times many applications for permission to convey persons and goods by private transport have been rejected by road transportation boards. The number recently has been higher owing to the care that has to be taken to ensure the conservation of petrol, oil, and rubber supplies.</li>

<li>(3) Every possible step has been taken.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Fall of Tobruk.</heading>

<question by="#le_roux" to="#prime_minister">

<num>X.</num>

<from>Mr. S. P. LE ROUX (for Mr. Louw)</from>

<p>asked the Prime Minister:</p>

</question>

<answer by="#prime_minister" to="#le_roux">

<from>The PRIME MINISTER:</from>

<p>Although no official report upon the fall of Tobruk has been published, I can give the assurance that the good name of the S.A. Commanding Officer or of his troops is not in any way involved. <heading>Price of Fruit.</heading>

<question by="#bertha_solomon" to="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry">

<num>XXIII.</num>

<from>Mrs. BERTHA SOLOMON</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture and Forestry:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether his attention has been drawn to a statement made by the Secretary of the Fruit and Vegetable Traders&#x2019; Association that the present high cost of fruit to the public is directly attributable to the Deciduous Fruit Board and that the Board is also responsible for wastage of fruit; and, if so,</li>

<li>(2) what action the Minister proposes to take in the matter.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry" to="#bertha_solomon">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE AND FORESTRY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes and I immediately called for a report from the Deciduous Fruit Board. The Board states that&#x2014;

<ol>

<li>(a) up to the present only peaches and plums have been available; pears and grapes are only just beginning to come in;</li>

<li>(b) while it controls all plums, peaches may be, and are, in fact, marketed by producers direct and the Board thus only handles the surplus peaches;</li>

<li>(c) it has been scientifically established that the eating quality of both plums and pears is improved by cold storage;</li>

<li>(e) no fruit has had to be destroyed and prices at auction are, and have been, reasonable although perhaps somewhat high during the early part of the season when supplies were still short.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

<heading>General Election.</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#prime_minister">

<from>Dr. MALAN, with leave, asked the Prime Minister:</from>

<p>Whether, in view of the general importance of the matter, he will inform the House&#x2014;</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) whether he intends introducing legislation to extend the life of the present House of Assembly; if not,</li>

<li>(2) whether he will give an indication as to the time when he intends having the general election held;</li>

<li>(3) whether such general election will be held in accordance with the new delimitation; if so, when will the report of the Delimitation Commission be published; and</li>

<li>(4) whether he intends having the general elections for the House of Assembly and the Provincial Councils held simultaneously and consequently to make an alteration to the statutory life of the present Provincial Councils.</li>

<p>Before calling upon the Leader of the Opposition (Dr. Malan) to move the motion of which he has given notice, I wish to draw attention to the fact that in dealing with the general question of &#x201C;social security&#x201D; it refers to a large number of social and economic problems. It will be entrusted with the task of reclassifying workers in industry, with the task of determining which classes of work are to be regarded as skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled. It will also be responsible for seeing to it that there is work for every man and woman in the country, and that those who do not want to work will be compelled to work&#x2014;a system which we already have in this country up to a certain extent.
