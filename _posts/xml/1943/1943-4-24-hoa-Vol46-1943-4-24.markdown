<heading>SECOND REPORT OF S.C. ON INTERNAL ARRANGEMENTS.</heading>

<p>Mr. SPEAKER, as Chairman brought up the Second Report of the Select Committee on Internal Arrangements, as follows:</p>

<block name="quote">Your Committee, having taken into consideration the question of the admission of strangers, begs to recommend that, in addition to the use of the Parliamentary Library under the Joint Rules adopted in 1913 (Standing Orders, Appendix I, Section 7), ex-members of the House of Assembly be allowed, on production of a ticket to be issued by Mr. Speaker, to enter the Lobby of the House of Assembly and to make use of the refreshment rooms (i.e., the dining room, coffee lounge and bar lounge), but <span class="col_6067-6068" refersTo="page_1373"/>that they be not allowed to invite other strangers into any part of the buildings.</block>

<p>E. G. Jansen, Chairman.</p>

<p>Report considered and adopted.</p>
