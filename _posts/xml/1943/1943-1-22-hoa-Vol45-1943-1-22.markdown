<heading>Miners&#x2019; Phthisis Pensions.</heading>

<question by="#miles-cadman" to="#minister_of_mines">

<num>IV.</num>

<from>Rev. MILES-CADMAN (for Mr. M. J. van den Berg)</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Mines:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he intends introducing legislation in regard to pensions for miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers and their dependants during the present session; if so,</li>

<li>(2) whether such pension benefits will apply to all sufferers from miners&#x2019; phthisis in its various stages as well as to their dependants;</li>

<li>(3) whether the present scale of pensions for miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers and their dependants will be increased; and</li>

<li>(4) whether miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers and their dependants are at present receiving cost of living allowances; if not, why not.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_mines" to="#miles-cadman">

<from>The MINISTER OF MINES:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (2) and (3) The Commission appointed by the Governor-General to inquire into the question of miners&#x2019; phthisis compensation has not yet reported. Until the Commission&#x2019;s Report has been considered by the Government it is not the intention to introduce any amending legislation affecting the <span class="col_233-234" refersTo="page_0192"/>payment of benefits to miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers. I do not think it will be possible to introduce any such amending legislation this Session.</li>

<li>(4) Yes, certain classes of miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers and their dependants are at present in receipt of cost of living allowances paid from funds provided by the Chamber of Mines and administered by the Miners&#x2019; Phthisis Board.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Blowfly Pest.</heading>

<question by="#viljoen" to="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry">

<num>VII.</num>

<from>Mr. D. T. DU P. VILJOEN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture and Forestry:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether the blowfly pest has been more severe during the past year than ever before, if so,</li>

<li>(2) whether this is due to the carcases being no longer poisoned;</li>

<li>(3) what is the estimated loss caused directly and indirectly by the blowfly per year;</li>

<li><span class="col_235-236" refersTo="page_0193"/>(4) whether, in view of the seriousness of the blowfly pest, the Government intends taking special steps for combating it; if so, what steps; and</li>

<li>(5) whether many farmers are changing over to cross-breds on account of the blowfly pest.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry" to="#viljoen">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE AND FORESTRY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) The blowfly menance was admittedly serious during the past season, but cannot be described generally as worse than ever before.</li>

<li>(2) No, but climate conditions, especially the moist atmosphere and green pasture which causes purging in sheep, were very favourable for the activity of blowflies.</li>

<li>(3) It is impossible to determine the loss occasioned in this manner.</li>

<li>(4) The Government is already doing its utmost to combat the pest, both by means of research and by affording guidance to farmers. As the Honourable member knows, a method of spraying to combat blowfly has been discovered and although initially certain difficulties were experienced in securing adequate supplies of the spraying material, large quantities have nevertheless been made available to farmers.</li>

<li>(5) I have no information in regard to the matter.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Refugees and Evacuees.</heading>

<question by="#viljoen" to="#minister_of_the_interior">

<num>X.</num>

<from>Mr. D. T. DU P. VILJOEN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of the Interior:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) How many refugees and evacuees are accommodated in the Union;</li>

<li>(2) how many of them hold positions in the Union; and</li>

<li>(3) what steps the Government intends taking after the war in connection with such persons.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry" to="#viljoen">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE AND FORESTRY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) and (2) The clerical assistance to collate the required information and to furnish accurate replies is not available. 13,419 refugees and evacuees have been admitted to the Union during the present war, but many of them have already departed either en route to England or returned to Egypt. No alien can take employment except on the authority of the Government and permission to accept employment has not been granted in many cases.</li>

<li>(3) All persons who entered by virtue of organised schemes remain the responsibility of their respective governments who will arrange for their repatriation after the war or earlier if the time is opportune. Persons who entered on their own initiative are required to comply with the laws applicable to immigrants and aliens. There has been no relaxation of the provisions of those laws. Aliens&#x2019; temporary permits are issued to all aliens who are required also to comply with the provisions of the Aliens&#x2019; Registration Act, 1939. There is no indication at present that there will be any necessity after the war for the Government to take steps in connection with the persons referred to other than the ordinary routine followed in the administration of the laws of the Union applicable to immigrants and aliens.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
