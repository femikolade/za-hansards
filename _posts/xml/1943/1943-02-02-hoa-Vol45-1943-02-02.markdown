<heading>Prisoners of War in Transit.</heading>

<p>The MINISTER OF DEFENCE replied to Question XVIII by Mr. Louw, standing over from 22nd January:</p>

<question by="#louw" to="#minister_of_defence">

<heading>Question:</heading>

<ol>

<li>(1) How many (a) Italian and German prisoners-of-war, (b) French internees, and (c) other Frenchmen are kept in custody in the Union at the expense of the British Government;</li>

<li><span class="col_739-740" refersTo="page_0445"/>(2) what has been the cost to date of keeping them in custody;</li>

<li>(3) what has been the cost to date of the housing, maintenance and training of the R.A.F. in South Africa; and</li>

<li>(4) what amount of the expenditure referred to in (2) and (3) above has been refunded by the British Government.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_defence" to="#louw">

<heading>Reply:</heading>

<ol>

<li>(1)

<ol>

<li>(a) 68,118 Italian and 501 German prisoners-of-war who are in transit.</li>

<li>(b) 191.</li>

<li>(c) 391, including women and children.<br/>In addition 1,699 Non-Europeans from Indo-China, and other French possessions are being detained as prisoners-of-war.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) So far as prisoners-of-war are concerned the actual cost of maintenance is calculated on a per capita basis which over the period of detention has varied from 1s. 6d. to 1s. 8d. per diem, and is being recovered from the United Kingdom Government. Recoveries to date amount to &#x00A3;2,236,464.</li>

<li>(b) (French Internees). &#x00A3;20,880 of which &#x00A3;721 has so far been recovered from United Kingdom Government.</li>

<li>(c) (Other French Subjects). All expenses are paid by the United Kingdom Government.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(3) It is not possible to state the cost of housing, feeding and training the R.A.F. as distinct from the S.A.A.F. as they work under a joint system, the Union Government being responsible for the housing and feeding of all members of both forces, while the United Kingdom Government is responsible for the provision of aircraft and other technical equipment used for training purposes. Pay and allowances are the responsibility of the respective Governments.</li>

<li>(4) The position is as reflected in (2) and (3) above.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
