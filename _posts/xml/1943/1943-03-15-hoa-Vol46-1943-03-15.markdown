<block name="quote">Accused, an attorney, had been committed and indicted for trial in the Circuit Court, Heilbron, for theft on twenty counts. C. R. An Indictment has been filed in this Court charging the accused, an officer of this Court, with the commission of serious offences, and the case is pending here.
