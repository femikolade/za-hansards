<heading>SPECIAL TAXATION AMENDMENT BILL.</heading>

<p>First Order read: Adjourned debate on Report stage, Special Taxation Amendment Bill, to be resumed.</p>

<p>[Debate adjourned on 19th April, when the following amendment had been moved by Mr. Davis: In Clause 13, in lines 14 and 15, to omit &#x201C;first day of October, 1939&#x201D; and to substitute &#x201C;twenty-sixth day of February, 1942&#x201D;.]</p>

<p>Amendment put and agreed to.</p>

<p>In Clause 16,</p>

<speech by="#minister_of_finance">

<from>The <person refersTo="hansard_za">MINISTER OF FINANCE</person>:</from>

<p>I move:</p>

<block name="quote">In Clause 16, in line 68, after &#x201C;into&#x201D; to insert &#x201C;before the commencement of the Special Taxation Amendment Act, 1943&#x201D;; and in lines 69, 70 and 71, to omit &#x201C;before the commencement of the Special Taxation Amendment Act, 1943&#x201D;.</block>

<p>This is not really consequential to the amendment which we have just accepted, but it has arisen out of the provisions of this Bill on the acceptance of this amendment. The point is this. The Minister also knows that to be the case and we on our side know that we cannot always believe what the Minister states here. His memory is playing him false. I have here before me a report of the Supreme Court for the year 1930. He said in the first instance&#x2014;</p>

<block name="quote">I have not the slightest hestitation in accepting the evidence of Coetzee in regard to what happened on that particular day.</block>
