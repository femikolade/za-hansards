That is the cost plus a percentage system, and that has been the experience of every country in the world during the war, that it is the worst system, and practically no country in the world was still employing it when the Commission sat. Then he does the work and there is no encouragement for him to push up the costs because he does not benefit from it. The third form is that when you want an air school built you call in a lot of contractors to the office and say: Look, we want an air school built which will cost approximately &#x00A3;250,000.
