I therefore want to ask the Prime Minister to reconsider this whole question. Fifteen miles from the coast as the crow flies is a long distance and quite a number of farmers will be cut out as a result of that decision. The farmers are looking to the Prime Minister and are appealing to him to place this labour at their disposal, and that is why I want to ask him again across the floor of this House to take the whole matter into review.</p>
