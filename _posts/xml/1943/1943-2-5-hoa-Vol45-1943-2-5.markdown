<p>First Report of Select Committee on Internal Arrangements.</p>

<p>Mr. SPEAKER, as Chairman, brought up the First Report of the Select Committee on Internal Arrangements, as follows:</p>

<block name="quote">Your Committee begs to report that, in consequence of the altered hours of the sittings of the House necessitated by the present &#x201C;dim-out&#x201D; conditions in Cape Town, it has reconsidered the hours during which the House of Assembly portion of the Parliamentary Buildings are kept open for the convenience of members. Your Committee considers that, in order to avoid any light being shown from the buildings between sunset and sunrise, it will be necessary to suspend the arrangement approved of in 1927 (V. and P., 1927, pp. 113-114), and recommends that under these circumstances the buildings should be closed at 8 p.m. every day, or at such other time as Mr. Speaker may direct.</block>

<p>E. G. Jansen, Chairman.</p>

<p>Mr. SPEAKER stated that unless notice of objection was given on or before Wednesday, the 10th February, 1943, the Report would be considered as adopted.</p>

<heading>Anti-Nagana Measures.</heading>

<question by="#egeland" to="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry">

<num>III.</num>

<from>Mr. EGELAND</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture and Forestry:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) How many instances of Nagana have been reported to his Department since 1st July, 1942, and in respect of which districts;</li>

<li>(2) what are the details of the agreement come to towards the end of 1942 between the Government, the Provincial Administration and the representatives of the Zululand settlers for putting into effect the policy decided upon at a conference held at Hluhluwe in June, 1941;</li>

<li>(3) what steps have been taken by his Department to expedite action in terms of such agreement;</li>

<li>(4) whether the Provincial Administration&#x2019;s decision to contribute financially to the carrying out of such agreement was made conditional upon the Government&#x2019;s defining its programme of game reduction and of other anti-Nagana measures in the areas under Government control excluding the Hluhluwe Reserve; and</li>

<li>(5) whether the Minister, appreciating the urgency of the situation, will make a full statement on the steps taken and to be taken in collaboration with the Provincial Administration and the Zululand settlers&#x2019; committee.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture_and_forestry" to="#egeland">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE AND FORESTRY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) The following new outbreaks have been reported:

<br/>District Eshowe: No. of outbreaks, cattle 1; No. of animals involved 10.<br/>District Lower Umfolozi: No. of outbreaks, cattle, 1; No. of animals involved, 80.<br/>District Melmoth: No. of outbreaks, cattle, 4; No. of animals involved, 194.<br/>District Hlabisa: No. of outbreaks, equines, 1; No. of animals involved, 3.<br/>District Nongoma: No. of outbreaks, equines, 1; No. of animals involved, 4.</li>

<li>(2) and (3) No policy was decided upon in 1941, but in consequence of a meeting in October 1942 between myself, the Minister of Posts and Telegraphs and representatives of various Departments, the Natal Provincial Administration and certain farmers&#x2019; associations, the following general policy was agreed upon, viz.&#x2014;

<ol>

<li>(a) the Hluhluwe game reserve to be maintained and the southern boundary thereof to be slightly extended and completely fenced to prevent migration of game southwards;</li>

<li>(b) high density breeding places of flies to be eliminated by clearing of bush and burning of veld and areas free of undergrowth and shrubs to be created to prevent migration of flies to settlements and native areas;</li>

<li>(c) trapping of flies in high density areas to be maintained;</li>

<li>(d) the corridor between Hluhluwe and Umfolozi game reserves to be kept free of game and cattle;</li>

<li>(e) game (other than white rhino) in areas outside the Hluhluwe game reserve to be gradually eliminated, the arrangements for this purpose being organised by a committee, consisting of representatives of the Department of Lands, Native Affairs and Agriculture and Forestry, the Natal Provincial Administration and farmers&#x2019; associations, under the chairmanship of the magistrate of Empangani.<br/>The actual elimination of game is intended to be undertaken in native areas by the Native Affairs Department, in the Umfolozi and Mkuzi reserves by my Department, and on occupied and unoccupied farms by the farmers who will for that purpose be enabled to secure the necessary rifles and ammunition on the recommendation of the magistrate.</li>

</ol></li>

<li><span class="col_1007-1008" refersTo="page_0579"/>(4) Negotiations between my Department and the Provincial Administration are still in progress and it is hoped that it will be possible to reach finality shortly.</li>

<li>(5) As indicated in (2) and (3) above, the Government is fully alive to the seriousness of the position, and it is hoped that the proposals outlined will eventually result in considerable improvement.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
