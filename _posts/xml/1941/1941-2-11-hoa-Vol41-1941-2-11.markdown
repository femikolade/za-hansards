<heading>Automatic Telephone Exchanges: Air-conditioning.</heading>

<question by="#neate" to="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs">

<num>I.</num>

<from>Mr. NEATE</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Posts and Telegraphs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) At how many auto-telephone exchanges in the Transvaal, Cape, Natal and Orange Free State, respectively, have air-conditioning plants been installed;</li>

<li>(2) how many of these plants are functioning satisfactorily, and which of them are not functioning satisfactorily;</li>

<li>(3) whether the technical officers of the Public Works Department and those of the Post Office differ as to the efficiency of certain plants;</li>

<li>(4) at what auto-exchanges have the air-conditioning plants been taken over by the Post Office, and what plants have been refused acceptance by the Post Office owing to alleged inefficiency; and</li>

<li>(5) what steps does the Minister propose to take to safeguard the health of the auto-maintenance staffs and to ensure that the conditions in which they work shall be the best obtainable, at every auto-telephone exchange in the country?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs" to="#neate">

<from><span class="col_2745-2746" refersTo="page_0505"/>The MINISTER OF POSTS AND TELEGRAPhS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) The number of air-conditioning plants installed at automatic exchanges is as follows:<br/>

Transvaal 15, Cape Province 12, Natal 6, Orange Free State 1. Total 34. In addition seven plants are under construction in the Transvaal.</li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) 25.</li>

<li>(b) Port Elizabeth, Central, Port Elizabeth North End, Hillbrow, City, Auckland Park, Brakpan and Benoni not yet satisfactory. Automatic equipment has not yet been installed at the remaining two exchanges, namely, Florida and Roodepoort.</li></ol></li>

<li>(3) No.</li>

<li>(4)

<ol>

<li>(a) Mayfair, Springs, Turffontein, Kensington, Germiston, Pretoria Central, Cape Town Central, Rondebosch, Wynberg, Muizenberg, Simonstown, Bellville, Parow, Port Elizabeth Central, Cape Road, Walmer, East London, Pietermaritzburg, Pinetown, Rossburgh, Malvern and Durban North have been taken over by Post Office.</li>

<heading>Corporal Bosch: Train Accident.</heading>

<question by="#van_coller" to="#minister_of_railways_and_harbours">

<num>III.</num>

<from>Mr. VAN COLLER</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Railways and Harbours:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether his attention has been drawn to a serious accident which happened recently to a certain Corporal Bosch, who, while travelling home by train, was leaning out of a carriage window shortly after the train left Queenstown Station, when his head was struck by the ironwork of a girder bridge, necessitating his removal to hospital;</li>

<li>(2) what are the reasons for such nonprosecution;</li>

<li>(3) whether ticks are increasing rapidly in that area;</li>

<li>(4) what investigations have been made into the effectiveness or otherwise of certain dips to kill the ticks, and what has been the result of such investigations;</li>

<li>(5) whether it has been ascertained by experiments that nicotine is the most effective dip to kill ticks which have become immune to other standard dips;</li>

<li>(6) what is the price per gallon of nicotine;</li>

<li>(7) whether representations have been made to subsidise the farmers to the extent of the increased cost of dipping until the experiments are complete; and</li>

<li>(8) whether the proposals made by the Divisional Council of East London have reached him; if so, what decision has he come to in connection therewith.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE AND FORESTRY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1), (2) and (3) Prosecutions were instituted, but the sentences imposed were subsequently annulled when it was found that the control of blue ticks by means of arsenical dips presented exceptional difficulty. In the circumstances, these ticks are probably on the increase.</li>

<li>(4) and (5) A number of mixtures have recently been tested extensively under field conditions with a certain measure of success, especially with nicotine, and further investigations are being carried out.</li>

<li>(6) The price ranges from &#x00A3;2 12s. 6d. to &#x00A3;5 per tin of ten pounds.</li>

<li>(7) Yes.</li>

<li>(8) Certain proposals have been received, but no decision has yet been reached in regard thereto.</li>

</ol>
