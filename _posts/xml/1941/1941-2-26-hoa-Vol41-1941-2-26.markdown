<speech by="#prime_minister">

<from>The <person refersTo="hansard_za">PRIME MINISTER</person>:</from>

<p>I move&#x2014;</p>

<block name="quote">That, until this House shall by resolution otherwise determine, the following be a sessional order governing the business of this House on Tuesdays and Fridays:</block>

<ol>

<li>(1) Tuesdays.&#x2014;On Tuesdays, on and after Tuesday, 11th March&#x2014;

<ol>

<li>(i) Questions shall have precedence for the remainder of the session;</li>

<li>(ii) Government business shall have precedence after questions, provided that private members shall be given the facilities for measures referred to in Standing Order No. 42 (3); and</li>

<li>(iii) the House shall suspend business at six o&#x2019;clock p.m. and resume at 8 o&#x2019;clock p.m.</li></ol></li>

<li>(2) Fridays.&#x2014;On Fridays, on and after Friday, 14th March&#x2014;

<ol>

<li>(i) Questions shall have precedence for the remainder of the session;</li>

<li>(ii) motions standing in the name of private members shall, subject to Standing Order No. 40, have precedence after questions; and</li>

<li>(iii) the House shall not suspend business at six o&#x2019;clock p.m. unless otherwise ordered.</li></ol></li></ol>

<p>The object of this motion is from the 11th March to take Tuesdays for Government business; certain facilities will, however, be granted for private members&#x2019; Bills which have passed through the preliminary stages. Private members&#x2019; Bills which have passed the second reading will be given the opportunity of being proceeded with so that they may be diposed of. For all other purposes, however, the Government is taking over Tuesdays. Friday then becomes motion day and the motions which on the Order Paper appear for Tuesdays will be put down for Friday afternoons with the suspension of the 6 o&#x2019;clock rule. I think it is reasonable in view of the advanced stage of the session that such a change should be made as from the 11th March, and that the Government should get more time for Government business. I move this resolution which I think is in the best interest of the country and which should have the approval of the other side of the House.</p>

</speech>

<p>In order to understand the position, we must know exactly what is laid down in the Standing Rules. The Standing Rules which provide what the rights of private members are in regard to private members&#x2019; days lay it down that Tuesdays will be private members&#x2019; days throughout the whole session. Unless the House should decide for some other good reason to depart from that practice, private members, however long the session may last, are entitled throughout the session to have Tuesdays, and then it is laid down in regard to the second private members&#x2019; day, namely, Friday, that private members shall be entitled to have Fridays until and including the 51st sitting day. The arrangement made by the House was that when it reached the 51st sitting day and Fridays would be taken away, Tuesdays were handed over entirely to the Government, and private members&#x2019; day which would have been on Tuesdays was moved to Friday. That was the practice of the House. Now this session was divided into two parts. The first part was last year, and the House sat for 19 days. The second part started on the 27th January of this year, and Friday, the 14th March, which is being proposed here as the day to be taken by the Government for Government business, will, if we take both parts of the session into account, be the 49th day. That is quite in order, because the following Friday would take us beyond the 51st day. To that extent we can raise no objections, but we must not forget that for the first 19 days, that is for the first part of the session, all private members&#x2019; days, Tuesdays and Fridays, were taken away from members, and, that being so, so far as members&#x2019; rights to private members&#x2019; days are concerned, we can take it that the session only started on the 27th January. In that connection the Prime Minister earlier on made a statement to the effect that he would again bring those private members&#x2019; days into force to which members were entitled. Well, the 11th March will not be the 51st sitting day, but, explained on the basis which I have put before the House, it will be the 30th day, so it amounts to this, that members, instead of getting the twenty private members&#x2019; days to which they were entitled this session, if we take Tuesdays and Fridays together, will only get twelve private members&#x2019; days if this motion is agreed to. In other words, private members will be deprived of eight private members&#x2019; days this session. Instead of getting twenty days, they will only get twelve&#x2014;in other words, they will be deprived of eight. The amendment which I am moving aims at rectifying matters, and if it is agreed to the position will be exactly as laid down by the Standing Rules and Orders. That motion deals with a subject which has for many years been occupying the minds of the farming population. Expectations have been roused in that connection, and people have been looking forward to a decision from the Government on the matter.
