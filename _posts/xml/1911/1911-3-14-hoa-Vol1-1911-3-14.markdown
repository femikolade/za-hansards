<debateSection name="#high_commissioner&#x2019;s_bill_message_to_the_senate">

<heading>HIGH COMMISSIONER&#x2019;S BILL.<br/>MESSAGE TO THE SENATE.</heading>

<speech by="#speaker">

<from>Mr. <person refersTo="hansard_za">SPEAKER</person></from>

<p>read the following message:</p>

<p>The House of Assembly transmits to the Honourable the Senate the Bill &#x201C;To provide for the appointment, duties, and remuneration of a High Commissioner for the Union of South Africa in the United Kingdom,&#x201D; in which the Honourable the Senate has made certain amendments in clauses 1 and 2.</p>

<p>The House of Assembly has concurred in the amendment in the Dutch version of clause 1, and has made endorsement thereof in the copy herewith sent.</p>

<p>The House of Assembly respectfully submits to the Honourable the Senate that the proposed omission of the words &#x201C;calculated at a rate&#x201D; in clause 2 could be construed as having the effect of an increase of expenditure by an allocation of the appropriation of public money, and such omission by the Honourable the Senate would in consequence be in conflict with the provisions of section 60 of the South Africa Act.</p>

<p>The House of Assembly therefore regrets that it is unable to agree to this amendment, and trusts that the Honourable the Senate will not insist upon it.</p>

<p>House of Assembly, 14th March, 1911.</p>

</speech>

<speech by="#speaker">

<from>Mr. <person refersTo="hansard_za">SPEAKER</person></from>

<p>added that yesterday only sub-section 3 of section 60 was referred to, but the correctness of the decision given was made absolutely clear by sub-section 2 of that clause, which said that the Senate may not amend any Bill so far as it imposed taxation or appropriated revenue or moneys for the service of the Government.</p>

</speech>
