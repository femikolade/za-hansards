<p>On new clause 1,</p>

</speech>

<speech by="#minister_of_justice">

<from>The <person refersTo="hansard_za">MINISTER OF JUSTICE</person></from>

<p>moved on page 2, lines 48 and 50, after &#x201C;of any&#x201D; to omit &#x201C;such&#x201D;; and on page 4, line 9, after &#x201C;salary&#x201D; to insert &#x201C;and local allowance (if any and for so long as such judge shall reside in such place as to which the said allowance shall apply).&#x201D; He said that when this section was before the House on the last occasion, it was pointed out by the hon. member for Cape Town, Harbour, and other members, that it was not advisable that a local allowance, temporarily granted to judges residing in Bloemfontein, the Transvaal, and Griqualand West, should be taken away after having once been granted. If this allowance could be taken away, it would put a judge under the influence of the Government in office for the time being. At the same time, it was pointed out that it was necessary to have the local allowance taken away in the case of a judge who ceased to reside in the district for which the local allowance was granted.</p>

</speech>

<speech by="#wilcocks">

<from>Mr. <person refersTo="hansard_za">C. T. M. WILCOCKS</person> (Fauresmith)</from>

<speech by="#juta">

<from>Sir <person refersTo="hansard_za">H. H. JUTA</person> (Cape Town, Harbour)</from>

<p>said that to carry out what the Minister had promised, and what he had to a great extent carried out, it would be necessary to delete certain other words. For instance, in line 7, the words &#x201C;for so long as the Governor-General may determine,&#x201D; appeared; these should come out. The committee had agreed that there should be a local allowance of &#x00A3;250, and it should not be within the power of the Governor-General to alter that. He moved that the words be deleted. He wished to point out to the Minister also that the words &#x201C;not exceeding&#x201D; appeared before the sum of &#x00A3;250. That meant that the Governor-General might make a judge an allowance of only &#x00A3;1, but the idea was that the judge should have an allowance of &#x00A3;250, and that he should not be placed in the power of the Governor-General. He moved that the words &#x201C;not exceeding&#x201D; be deleted, and &#x201C;of&#x201D; substituted.</p>

</speech>
