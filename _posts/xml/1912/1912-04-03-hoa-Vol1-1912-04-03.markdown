<p>Clause 21 was the only clause which was of any consequence at all to people living on the east side of the Rand, and it seemed to him that these rights were taken away not because the Government needed them, but because of something which might happen in future. There was an abundance of good coal yet the Government wished to get at the land owners and say that these were their (the Government&#x2019;s) rights, and not the land owners&#x2019;. The neighbouring man who might have a coal seam on his farm was left alone, and yet the question was the same&#x2014;getting hold of the power. It was not the water they wanted, but the power.
