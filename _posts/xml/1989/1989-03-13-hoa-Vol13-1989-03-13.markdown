<heading>Language Monument Fund: reserve amount</heading>

<question by="#gerber" to="#minister_of_national_education">

<num>53.</num>

<from>Mr A GERBER</from>

<p>asked the Minister of National Education:&#x2020;</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether, after the erection of the Language Monument in Paarl, there was a reserve amount in the Language Monument Fund; if so, what was this amount;</li>

<li>(2) whether any allocations have been made to institutions from this reserve fund; if so, (a) to which institutions, (b) what amounts, (c) for what purpose, and (d) when, in each case;</li>

<li>(3) whether there is a body which exercises control over this fund; if so, (a) what body, (b) who are the members of this body, (c) (i) by whom, (ii) when and (iii) for what period have they been appointed and (d) to whom do they report;</li>

<li>(4) what is the current position of the fund?</li>

</ol>

<p>B146E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_national_education" to="#gerber">

<from>The MINISTER OF NATIONAL EDUCATION:</from>

<p>The &#x201C;Afrikaanse Taalmonumentfonds&#x201D;, which consisted of contributions collected by a committee, was used to establish the Afrikaans Language Monument. After the handing over of the Afrikaans Language Monument and the Afrikaans Language Museum to the State in 1977, the &#x201C;Afrikaanse Taalfonds&#x201D; was established. The &#x201C;Fonds&#x201D; <i>inter alia</i> administers the remaining funds of the &#x201C;Afrikaanse Taalmonumentfonds&#x201D;. The &#x201C;Fonds&#x201D; is an organisation that administers its affairs in terms of its own constitution. Particulars regarding the &#x201C;Afrikaanse Taalfonds&#x201D; and its activities should therefore be obtained from that organisation itself.</p>

</answer>
