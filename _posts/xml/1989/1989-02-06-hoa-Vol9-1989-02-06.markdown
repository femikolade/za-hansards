<p>Nevertheless I believe that all of us will also agree with this point: We realise that peace cannot be preserved as an absolute value. <p>We talk about reasonableness. We agree with the appeal of the hon the Acting State President for reasonableness, and with his warning against emotionalism. Our brain cells tell us that if one&#x2019;s blood cells are not working, one&#x2019;s brain cells will soon not be working either. <p>Reference is made to senseless infighting. The hon the Acting State President is impatient with senseless infighting.
