<heading>Universities: subsidy formula</heading>

<question by="#burrows" to="#minister_of_national_education">

<num>22.</num>

<from>Mr R M BURROWS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of National Education:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he has determined the formula for the subsidy of each university in the Republic; if not, why not; if so, what is this formula;</li>

<li>(2) what value of <i>a</i> in terms of the subsidy formula has he determined for each university in the Republic for the (a) 1986, (b) 1987, (c) 1988 and (d) 1989 university financial years;</li>

<li>(3) whether in any case the value of <i>a</i> was less than one; if so, why;</li>

<li>(4) whether different universities had different <i>a</i> values in the same year; if so, why;</li>

<li>(5) whether he will make a statement on the matter?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_national_education" to="#burrows">

<from>The MINISTER OF NATIONAL EDUCATION:</from>

<li>(1) Yes. General Policy in respect of norms and standards for the financing of the running and capital costs of universities was determined on 27 October 1987.
