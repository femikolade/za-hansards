<heading>Television satellite dishes: restrictions</heading>

<question by="#burrows" to="#minister_of_communications">

<num>138.</num>

<from>Mr R M BURROWS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Communications:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether there are any restrictions on private persons and companies owning and/or using television satellite dishes; if so, what restrictions;</li>

<li>(2) whether any person or authority licenses the private ownership of television satellite dishes; if so, (a) what person or authority and (b) (i) how many have been so licensed and (ii) in respect of what specified period is this information furnished;</li>

<li>(3) whether any applications have been received for the private use of television receiving installations by private persons and/or companies; if so, how many such applications were (a) received and (b) granted;</li>

<li>(4) whether he will make a statement on the matter?</li>

</ol>

<p>B337E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_communications" to="#burrows">

<from>The MINISTER OF COMMUNICATIONS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes; if such dishes can be used for the transmission and/or reception of signals relayed via satellites;</li>

<li>(2) yes;

<ol>

<li>(a) the Postmaster General,</li>

<li>(b)

<ol>

<li>(i) one, namely the South African Broadcasting Corporation, and</li>

<li>(ii) from 12 June 1986 to date;</li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

<li>(3) yes;

<ol>

<li>(a) approximately 45,</li>

<li>(b) none;</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(4) not at this stage. In view of the interest displayed in privately-owned earth stations and with due regard to developments in other countries, the Department is studying the entire matter. A statement will be issued when the time is appropriate.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
