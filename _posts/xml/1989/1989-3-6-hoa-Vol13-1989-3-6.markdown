<heading>Agricultural products: shortages</heading>

<question by="#lorimer" to="#minister_of_agriculture">

<num>29.</num>

<from>Mr R J LORIMER</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether there are any shortages of agricultural products at present or any such shortages are expected in 1989; if so, of what products;</li>

<li>(2) what in each case are the (a) reasons for and (b) price implications of these shortages;</li>

<li>(3) what steps are being taken or are contemplated in each case to counter the effects of these shortages?</li>

</ol>

<p>B84E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture" to="#lorimer">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes, in addition to agricultural products such as rice, tea, coffee, cocoa-beans and certain grades and classes of cotton and tobacco in respect of which the Republic is not self-sufficient, there are at present shortages of red meat and sunflower seed. Shortages of these products are also expected during 1989.</li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) The shortages can be ascribed mainly to abnormal climatic conditions.</li>

<li>(b) No significant price implications are expected in respect of sunflower seed. The shortage of red meat is being replenished to a certain extent through imports, but meat prices nevertheless rose considerably and it is expected that price levels will remain relatively high during 1989.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(3) The effects of these shortages will be countered to a large extent by means of imports.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
