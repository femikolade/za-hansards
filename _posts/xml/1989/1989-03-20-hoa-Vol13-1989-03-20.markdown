<heading>State hospitals: integrated wards</heading>

<question by="#barnard" to="#minister_of_national_health_and_population_development">

<num>20.</num>

<from>Dr M S BARNARD</from>

<p>asked the Minister of National Health and Population Development:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether any wards in hospitals administered by the State are integrated; if so, how many in each specified hospital; if not, why not;</li>

<li>(2) whether his Department intends to desegregate wards in State hospitals; if not, why not; if so, when;</li>

<li>(3) whether any studies have been carried out into the cost implications of desegregating wards in State hospitals; if not, why not; if so, (a) when, (b) by whom and (c) what were the findings;</li>

<li>(4) whether any wards in State hospitals are under-utilized; if so, (a) in which specified hospitals and (b) to what extent;</li>

<li>(5) whether any wards in State hospitals are overcrowded; if so, (a) in which specified hospitals and (b) to what extent?</li>

</ol>

<p>B74E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_national_health_and_population_development" to="#barnard">

<from>The MINISTER OF NATIONAL HEALTH AND POPULATION DEVELOPMENT:</from>

<p>The honourable member is referred to my written reply to Question 404 on 30 March 1988.</p>

</answer>
