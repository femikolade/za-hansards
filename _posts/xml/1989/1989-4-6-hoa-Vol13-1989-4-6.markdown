<heading>SAP members employed in Greater Pietermaritzburg area</heading>

<question by="#ellis" to="#minister_of_law_and_order">

<num>48.</num>

<from>Mr M J ELLIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Law and Order:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) How many members of the South African Police Force are currently employed in the Greater Pietermaritzburg area, (b) where are they stationed, (c) in which areas are they deployed and (d) in respect of what date is this information furnished;</li>

<li>(2) whether any such members have been involved in (a) intimidation, (b) eliciting food or property, (c) assault, (d) assault with intent to do grievous bodily harm, (e) murder, (f) rape and (g) culpable homicide; if so, (i) (aa) what action has been taken against them, and (bb) when, and (ii) what was the outcome of such action, in each case?</li>

</ol>

<p>B100E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_law_and_order" to="#ellis">

<from>The MINISTER OF LAW AND ORDER:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) to (d) It is neither in the interest of the public nor in the interest of security to divulge the numerical strength of the South African Police in a specific area or at specific police stations.</li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) No</li>

<li>(b) No</li>

<li>(c) Yes</li>

<li>(d) No</li>

<li>(e) No</li>

<li>(f) No</li>

<li>(g) No

<ol>

<li>(i) (aa) and (bb) After a case is reported to the Police, a case docket is registered and investigated. If the perpetrator(s) is/ are known, on completion of the investigation the case docket is submitted to the Attorney-General for a decision.</li>

<li>(ii) In one case the Attorney-General instituted legal proceedings and the police official concerned was convicted and sentenced.<br/>At present 29 cases are still being investigated by the Police.<br/>In 73 cases the Atorney-General refused to institute legal proceedings and the case dockets concerned were closed as &#x201C;withdrawn&#x201D;.<br/>On completion of the investigation, in 7 cases it was found that the complaints were false and the case dockets concerned were closed by a police officer as &#x201C;false&#x201D;.<br/>In 36 cases, perpetrators who were alleged to have been responsible for some of the assaults could not be detected and the case dockets concerned were closed by a police officer as &#x201C;undetected&#x201D;.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>

<li>&#x2014; Meaningful utilization of time which is aimed at positive orientation after release.</li>

<li>&#x2014; Maintenance of present social structures to counter-act institutionalization and so doing prevent recidivism e.g. maintenance and strengthening of family ties.</li>

<li>&#x2014; The fulfilment of religious needs.</li>

<li>&#x2014; Healthy physical development through physical training and care.</li>
