<heading>Group Areas Act: complaints investigated by SAP</heading>

<question by="#van_der_merwe" to="#minister_of_law_and_order">

<num>15.</num>

<from>Mr S S VAN DER MERWE</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Law and Order:</p>

<p>(a) How many complaints regarding offences in terms of the Group Areas Act were investigated by the South African Police in (i) the Western Cape Division and (ii) the Republic and (b) what was the outcome of each of these investigations (i) during the latest specified period of 12 months for which information is available and (ii) in 1988?</p>

<p>B69E</p>

</question>

<from>The MINISTER OF LAW AND ORDER:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a)

<ol>

<li>(i) 322 complaints</li>

<li>(ii) 1 641 complaints</li>

</ol></li>
