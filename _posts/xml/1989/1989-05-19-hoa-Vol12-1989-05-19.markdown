<p>During the past few years so much has been expected of the consumer. <p>In the constituency of Ashton I got a so-called Coloured to make an investment. I asked him to purchase the hotel complex there and to build a hotel with other facilities for our people. <p>While the complex was being built, a White was allowed to open a liquor store a few yards away. That Coloured man with that big complex, which at the moment provides work for 36 people, and the number can increase to 60, was refused an off-sales licence, whereas the White a few yards away was allowed to carry on with his business undisturbed, although he only employs two people. Only two! Hon members can now see what dishonesty and swindling is taking place. I also want to tell hon members that if that friend of mine were to close down that complex, 36 people would be without a job.</p>

<p>We have just received a survey made by the Reserve Bank on the foreign debt position as it was at the end of 1988. <p>At the end of 1988 the value of the total foreign debt was equal to only $21 billion, of which only $9 billion or only 43&#x0025; was inside and $12 billion outside the net.</p>

<p>These figures do not reflect the full effect of the debt repayment that has been made since 1985. A net decrease in the exchange rate of the dollar against other currencies over this period increased the dollar value of the non-dollar debt.</p>

<p>Naturally this situation fluctuates from day to day, because we have a whole basket of exchange rates in our foreign debt basket.</p>

<p>Over this period South Africa has repaid approximately $2 billion of the debt within the net.
