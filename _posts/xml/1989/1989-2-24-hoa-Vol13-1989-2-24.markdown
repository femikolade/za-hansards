<heading>Sandton: applications from non-Whites to occupy/own property</heading>

<question by="#dalling" to="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning">

<num>24.</num>

<from>Mr D J DALLING</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Constitutional Development and Planning:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) How many applications were received in 1988 from (a) Coloured, (b) Indian and (c) Black persons to (i) occupy and (ii) own residential property in areas proclaimed for occupation by White persons in Sandton;</li>

<li>(2) how many such applications (a) had been (i) granted and (ii) refused and (b) were pending as at the above date?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning" to="#dalling">

<from>The MINISTER OF CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING:</from>

<p>This matter vests in the Administrator of the Transvaal andhe furnished the following information:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) None.</li>

<li>(2) Falls away.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
