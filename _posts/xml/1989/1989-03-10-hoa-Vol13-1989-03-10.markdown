<p>What estimated number of (a) Whites, (b) Coloureds and (c) Indians resided in the Greater Cape Town area as at 31 December 1988?</p>

<p>B162E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_home_affairs" to="#eglin">

<from>The MINISTER OF HOME AFFAIRS:</from>

<p>Information for 1988 on the basis as required by the hon member is not available in my Department. Population numbers are obtained by means of population censuses. The latest available information therefore, is that in respect of the 1985-Population Census. In this regard I refer the hon member to my reply to Question No 548 of 11 March 1988 which appeared under his name on the Question Paper.</p>

</answer>

<question by="#walsh" to="#minister_of_education_and_development_aid">

<num>126.</num>

<from>Mr J J WALSH</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Education and Development Aid:</p>

<p>Whether any land made available for occupation by Blacks since 1936 has subsequently been excised or is to be excised from self-governing territories or independent Black states; if so, (a) what area of land, (b) which such territory or state was involved, and (c) when, in each case?</p>

<p>B279E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_education_and_development_aid" to="#walsh">

<from>The MINISTER OF EDUCATION AND DEVELOPMENT AID:</from>

<p>Yes. The required information, however, is not readily available. No special record in the form of a register is kept in this connection and the required information cannot be ascertained without performing a considerable volume of work, which is deemed unjustified.</p>

</answer>
