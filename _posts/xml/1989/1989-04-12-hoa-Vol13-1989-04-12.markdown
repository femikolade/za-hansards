<heading>Renamo bases: persons involved</heading>

<question by="#rabie" to="#minister_of_foreign_affairs">

<num>9.</num>

<from>Mr J A RABIE</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Foreign Affairs:&#x2020;</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he instructed his Department to investigate the alleged involvement of three Black and two White South Africans at Renamo bases in the Gaza province in 1988; if so,</li>

<li>(2) whether any South Africans were involved at these bases; if so, (a) what was the nature of their involvement and (b) on whose behalf were they so involved;</li>

<li>(3) whether any of these South Africans were killed; if so, what are the relevant details;</li>

<li>(4) whether he will make a statement on the matter?</li>

</ol>

<p>C56E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_foreign_affairs" to="#rabie">

<from>The MINISTER OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS:</from>

<p>(1) No. I and my Department do not have at our disposal particulars which correspond to the incident as described by the Honourable Member. (2), (3) and (4) fall away.</p>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>

</ol>

<p>C29E</p>

<li>(1) No</li>

<li>(2) No; due to the physical limitations of the area</li>

<li>(3) No; vacant land not available (a) and (b) fall away</li>

<li>(4) No.</li>

</ol>
