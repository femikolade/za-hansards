<heading>Mossgas: foreigners employed on projects</heading>

<p>1. Mr J V IYMAN asked the Minister of Economic Affairs and Technology:</p>

<p>Whether he will make a statement on the (a) number of foreigners employed on Mossgas projects and (b) job categories involved?</p>

<speech by="#minister_of_economic_affairs_and_technology">

<from>The <person refersTo="hansard_za">MINISTER OF ECONOMIC AFFAIRS AND TECHNOLOGY</person>:</from>

<p>Mr Chairman, please accept my apology for being a bit late in answering this interpellation.</p>

<p>It is first of all a statistically difficult question to answer absolutely correctly. However, I will try to give as much information as is available from our statistical records.</p>

<p>The point that I have to make right at the beginning is that it is an accepted policy that we are looking at a local content of not less than 80&#x0025; to 85&#x0025;&#x2014;that is my target. I think it stands at about 75&#x0025; at the moment. Just about all of the semi-skilled, unskilled and skilled labour will be local labour.</p>

<p>Firstly, I will now give a summary of the manpower position on the off-shore contract. The figures are as follows: For project management we have 144 persons, for engineering 104, and for fabrication 919. That is as far as staffing is concerned. As far as supervisors and engineers are concerned, we have 36 for project management, 34 for engineering and 60 for fabrication.</p>
