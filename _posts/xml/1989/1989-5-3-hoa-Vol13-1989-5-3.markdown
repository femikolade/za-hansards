<heading><span class="col_961-962" refersTo="page_0520"/>NP: announcement of retirement of leader</heading>

<question by="#schoeman" to="#minister_of_information_broadcasting_services_and_the_film_industry">

<num>340.</num>

<from>Mr C B SCHOEMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Information, Broadcasting Services and the Film Industry:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he or any official in his Department released the announcement of the retirement of the leader of the National Party in South Africa; if so, (a) on whose authority and (b) why;</li>

<li>(2) whether he or his Department arranged a press conference to be addressed by the new leader of the National Party; if so, (a) on whose authority, (b) why and (c) at what cost?</li>

</ol>

<p>B695E</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_information_broadcasting_services_and_the_film_industry" to="#schoeman">

<from>The MINISTER OF INFORMATION, BROADCASTING SERVICES AND THE FILM INDUSTRY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) No.

<ol>

<li>(a) and (b) Fall away.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) No. The Bureau for Information did, however, hear that a press conference would take place later that day and in the light of the great national and international interest, this information was relayed to members of the media.

<ol>

<li>(a) This was done on the authority of the Minister of Information, Broadcasting Services and the Film Industry;</li>

<li>(b) In the light of the national and international interest in the event;</li>

<li>(c) None.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>
