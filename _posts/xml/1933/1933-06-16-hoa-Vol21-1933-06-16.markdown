<heading>Bruma Sewage Disposal.</heading>

<question by="#tothill" to="#minister_of_public_health">

<num>I.</num>

<from>Mr. TOTHILL</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Public Health:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he has received a copy of a report on the Bruma Sewage Disposal Works, Kensington, Johannesburg; and</li>

<li>(2) whether, in view of the nuisance and danger to public health caused by the continuance of these works on the present site, he proposes to take any action; if so, what action?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_public_health" to="#tothill">

<from>The MINISTER OF PUBLIC HEALTH:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) The department is aware that smells arising from the Bruma sewage disposal works, Johannesburg, have from time to time been complained of by the residents of Kensington, and the chief health officer was consulted some months ago by the city council of Johannesburg in regard to the appointment of an independent expert to investigate and report upon the position.</li>

<li>(2) It has not yet been established that any danger to public health is being caused by the continuance of these works on the present site and it is hoped that as a result of experiments <span class="col_1067-1068" refersTo="page_0544"/>that are being carried out by the city council, the undoubted nuisance that has at times existed will disappear or, at least, be greatly minimised. Under the circumstances, the department is merely keeping in touch with the city health department in regard to the matter and does not propose to take any special action unless its assistance is sought by the city council.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Broadcasting in Afrikaans.</heading>

<question by="#erasmus" to="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs">

<num>V.</num>

<from>Mr. ERASMUS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Posts and Telegraphs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) How manny announcers are employed in the Cape Town Broadcasting station;</li>

<li>(2) how many of them are capable of announcing efficiently (a) in English, (b) in Afrikaans, (c) in both languages; and</li>

<li>(3) how many hours per week are announcements made in (a) English, (b) Afrikaans?</li>

</question>

<ol>

S.O.S. intimations, market reports, weather reports, and all important services which can be regarded as falling under the heading of &#x201C;service&#x201D; announcements are broadcast in both official languages by two announcers. <heading>Miners&#x2019; Phthisis.</heading>

<p>The MINISTER OF MINES replied to Question XIV, by Maj. Roberts, standing over from 9th June.</p>

<question by="#roberts" to="#minister_of_mines">

<heading>Question:</heading>

<ol>

<li>(1) What is the present number of miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers;</li>

<li>(2) how many of them are drawing pensions;</li>

<li>(3) how many of those drawing pensions are employed by the gold mines, and at what wages;</li>

<li>(4) how many of those not drawing pensions are employed by the gold mines;</li>

<li>(5) how many of them are employed by municipalities and the Government, and at what wages;</li>

<li>(6) how many of them are unemployed;</li>

<li>(7) how many are unfit for employment and are not drawing pensions;</li>

<li>(8) what is the average yearly expenditure on miners&#x2019; phthisis;</li>

<li>(9) how many miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers die each year;</li>

<li>(10) how many miners have developed miners&#x2019; phthisis since gold-mining was started on the Witwatersrand;</li>

<li>(11) how many of them have died to date;</li>

<li>(12) what is the number of dependents of miners&#x2019; phthisis sufferers under (10) and (11);</li>

<li>(13) what percentage of the profits earned since gold-mining was started has been paid by the gold mines into the Treasury;</li>

<li>(14) what is the amount of the profits which have been made by the gold mines; and</li>

<li>(15) what proportion of the gold production of the mines has been expended on miners&#x2019; phthisis?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_mines" to="#roberts">

<heading>Reply:</heading>

<ol>

<li>(1) 7,595.</li>

<li>(2) 1,542.</li>

<li>(3) 146 at an average wage of 17s. per shiftplus overtime.</li>

<li>(4) 709 beneficiaries and 209 others who have not applied for and are not in receipt of benefits.</li>

<li>(5) According to the board&#x2019;s records, approximately 200, the majority of whom are employed <span class="col_1071-1072" refersTo="page_0546"/>on relief works at 6s. per day. There are no doubt others of whom the board has no record.</li>

<li>(6) According to the board&#x2019;s records, approximately 700, but again there may be others of whom the board has no record.</li>

<li>(7) No details are available and it is impossible on account of age, physical disability (other than miners&#x2019; phthisis), character, etc., to give even an approximate figure.</li>

<li>(8) &#x00A3;877,739 reckoned over the last eight years.</li>

<li>(9) The average number of deaths of miners&#x2019; phthisis beneficiaries reported to the board annually is 375. There is no record of others.</li>

<li>(10) It is impossible to say, but the board has record of 16,565 cases.</li>

<li>(11) The board has records of 8,761 deaths among the above-mentioned miners.</li>

<li>(12) There are approximately 36,801 dependents of the miners included in the figures given under (10) and (11).</li>

<li>(13) Approximately 17.36 per cent. for the period 1902 to 1932.</li>

<li>(14) The gross profits for the same period amounted to approximately &#x00A3;333,353,755. The figures given in (13) and (14) do not include claim licence moneys and mynpacht dues which are not related to profits. Figures relating to republican production are not given as they cannot be correlated to gold-mining taxation which was first introduced in 1902.</li>

<li>(15) Approximately 1.8 per cent. over the period May, 1911 (when miners&#x2019; phthisis legislation was first introduced) to December, 1932.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
