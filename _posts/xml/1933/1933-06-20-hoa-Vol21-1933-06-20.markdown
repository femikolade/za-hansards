<heading>Supreme Court, Maritzburg.</heading>

<question by="#o&#x2019;brien" to="#minister_of_labour">

<num>V.</num>

<from>Mr. O&#x2019;BRIEN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Public Works why no provision is being made on <span class="col_1295-1296" refersTo="page_0658"/>the loan estimates for additions and alterations to the Supreme Court, Maritzburg, which have been urged by judges and counsel in Natal?</p></question>

<answer by="#minister_of_labour" to="#o&#x2019;brien">

<from>The MINISTER OF LABOUR (for the Minister of Public Works):</from>

<p>It was not possible to provide for this service on this year&#x2019;s loan estimates owing to many other more urgent demands on the funds which could be made available. The necessity for additions and alterations to the Supreme Court, Pietermaritzburg, is fully appreciated, however, and plans are in hand now so as to ensure that the work can be proceeded with early next year as soon as funds are provided.</p>

</answer>

<oralStatements>

<heading>Farmers: Seed-wheat.</heading>

<question by="#swart" to="#minister_of_labour">

<num>VI.</num>

<from>Mr. SWART</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Labour in what areas, in what circumstances and under what conditions his department will supply indigent farmers with seed-wheat during the next few months of the sowing season?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_labour" to="#swart">

<from>The MINISTER OF LABOUR:</from>

<p>In consequence of the serious conditions caused by the prolonged drought in the Calvinia, Williston and Kenhardt districts, in which long distances render relief by means of road works impracticable, and rains having fallen, the Department of Labour authorized magistrates to make limited issues of seed-wheat against promissory notes upon the recommendation of responsible local authorities. Similar action has been taken in Namaqualand with a view to relieving distress and reducing the large numbers now employed on relief works. Where issues are made to a family, one or more members of which are employed on relief works, 25 per cent. of the daily wage will be deducted in repayment. It is not intended to extend this assistance to other areas.</p>

</answer>

</oralStatements>

<oralStatements>

<heading>Farmers: Seed-wheat.</heading>

<question by="#swart" to="#minister_of_agriculture">

<num>VII.</num>

<from>Mr. SWART</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture whether the Government is prepared to grant assistance to co-operative societies which supply their members with seed-wheat on credit and which are faced with a loss on the transaction as a result of failure of crops or other unexpected failure?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture" to="#swart">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE:</from>

<p>There is no reason to believe that, in the event of losses occurring, they will be so large that co-operative societies will not be able to recover at least the same quantity of seed-wheat as was issued to their members.</p>

</answer>

<oralStatements>

<heading>Butter Export.</heading>

<question by="#swart" to="#minister_of_agriculture">

<num>VIII.</num>

<from>Mr. SWART</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether representations were made to him in connection with the compulsory export quota for butter, for the purpose of putting a stop to the compulsory exportation or lowering the quota, in view of an expected shortage in the Union in the near future;</li>

<li>(2) whether there is ground for the apprehension that a shortage may occur, by which creameries will be compelled to import butter in order to comply with their standing contracts; and, if so,</li>

<li>(3) what steps he intends to take in order to prevent that state of affairs?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture" to="#swart">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) As soon as the Dairy Industry Control Board had an indication of a possible scarcity of butter as a result of the drought, steps were taken to suspend all compulsory export excepting the quotas for January, 1933.</li>

<li>(2) It is impossible at this stage to say whether there will be a shortage. The taking of further steps will depend upon circumstances.</li>

<li>(3) Falls away.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
