<heading>Community Development Board/Land Affairs Board: land purchased</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_public_works_and_land_affairs">

<num>785.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Public Works and Land Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) How much land was purchased by (i) the Community Development Board and (ii) any other specified board or body falling under his Department for use by Indians, Coloureds and Blacks, respectively, in each of the latest specified five years for which information is available, (b) what was the cost of the land so purchased in each of the above categories and (c) subject to what conditions was this land made available;</li>

<li>(2) whether the Land Affairs Board has come into operation; if not, (a) why not and (b) when is it anticipated that it will come into operation; if so,</li>

<li>(3) whether the latter board has purchased any land for use by the above population groups; if so, what are the relevant details?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_public_works_and_land_affairs" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF PUBLIC WORKS AND LAND AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) (i) The Department of Public Works and Land Affairs does not have the information at its disposal. The functions of the Community Development Board have been assigned to the three administrations for own affairs while the functions of the National Housing Commission have been assigned to the Department of Development Planning as well as to the three administrations for own affairs.

<ol>

<li>

<ol>

<li>(ii) None</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(b) and (c) Fall away.</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(2) No.

<ol>

<li>(a) Consultation in terms of section 3(1) of the Land Affairs Act, 1987 (Act 101 of 1987), had to take place.</li>

<li>(b) 1 August 1988.</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(3) Falls away.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Sheltered employment: salary parity</heading>

<question by="#george" to="#minister_of_manpower">

<num>18.</num>

<from>Mr T R GEORGE</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Manpower:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) What procedure is followed in determining the salaries of (a) White, (b) Coloured and (c) Indian persons employed in sheltered employment facilities;</li>

<li>(2) whether salary parity has been attained in respect of Whites, Coloureds and Indians employed in such facilities; if not, (a) why not and (b) when is it anticipated that parity will be attained;</li>

<li>(3) whether he will make a statement on the matter?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_manpower" to="#george">

<from>The MINISTER OF MANPOWER:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a), (b) and (c)<br/>

<p>The salaries of employees who are employed under the sheltered employment scheme are coupled to the salary scales of the occupational class maintenance officer (post class factotum) in the Public Service on condition that the salaries for each population group must correspond with the gradings per population group for the post class factotum.</p>

</li>

<li>(2) No.

<ol>

<li>(a) Attention is being given to the attainment of parity for employees under the sheltered employment scheme.</li>

<li>(b) Once the necessary approval has been obtained and funds become available.</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(3) No.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
