<heading>Amounts spent on housing</heading>

<question by="#soal" to="#minister_of_local_government_housing_and_works">

<num>4.</num>

<from>Mr P G SOAL</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Local Government, Housing and Works:</p>

<p> What amounts were spent by his Department in the 1987-88 financial year on the construction of housing for Whites in (a) Cape Town, (b) Durban, (c) Pietermaritzburg, (d) Pretoria, (e) Port Elizabeth, (f) Kimberley, (g) East London, (h) Bloemfontein and (i) Johannesburg?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_local_government_housing_and_works" to="#soal">

<from>The MINISTER OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT, HOUSING AND WORKS:</from>

<p>Amounts spent per metropolitan area for the period 1 April 1987 until 31 December 1987 are as follows:</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) R6 985 122</li>

<li>(b) R9 709 319</li>

<li>(c) R1 276 951</li>

<li>(d) R1 914 733</li>

<li>(e) R2 300 977</li>

<li>(f) R Nil</li>

<li>(g) R2 320 727</li>

<li>(h) R3 148 945</li>

<li>(i) R15 382 759</li>

</ol>

</answer>
