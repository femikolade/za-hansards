<heading>Price of diesel fuel: increase</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_economic_affairs_and_technology">

<num>525.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Economic Affairs and Technology:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether the price of diesel fuel was increased in 1987; if so, what was the extent of the increase;</li>

<li>(2) whether this increase was enacted separately from other fuel price increases or decreases; if so, why?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_economic_affairs_and_technology" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF ECONOMIC AFFAIRS AND TECHNOLOGY:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes, in certain instances. Various changes had an impact on diesel fuel prices which became effective on 1 July 1987, namely,

<ol>

<li>(a) a general decrease in the basic price before taxes, levies and transport costs as a result of lower landed costs, and the phasing out of the cumulative overrecovery on diesel by moving into a unit-underrecovery position on diesel and a reduction of the Equalisation Fund levy on diesel;</li>

<li>(b) an increase in rail tariffs with effect from 1 July 1987;</li>

<li>(c) an increase of <i>2c/l</i> in the National Road Fund Levy at that date; and</li>

<li>(d) a pooling of levies and taxes, inclusive of general sales tax, in a uniform fuel levy together with the introduction of a refund system for certain categories of diesel consumers; and</li>

<li>(e) the restructuring of the price grids.<br/>Consumers who did not qualify for rebates on taxes and levies experienced generally a price decrease of an average of 3c/<i>l</i> except in the Northern and North Eastern Transvaal where the elimination of the cross subsidisation of transport costs in these areas had the effect of increasing prices.<br/>Certain consumers who obtained rebated diesel for productive purposes also experienced increases as a result of the application of the refund system in as much that general sales tax is not refundable in respect of certain diesel applications.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) No.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
