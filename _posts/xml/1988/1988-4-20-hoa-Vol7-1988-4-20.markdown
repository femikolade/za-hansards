<heading>Post Office staff: cuts</heading>

<question by="#ellis" to="#minister_of_communications">

<num>667.</num>

<from>Mr M J ELLIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Communications:</p>

<p>Whether there are to be any cuts in Post Office staff in 1988; if so, (a) what percentage of the staff complement is it intended will (i) be retrenched and/or (ii) have their posts frozen and (b) how are these cuts to be effected?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_communications" to="#ellis">

<from>The MINISTER OF COMMUNICATIONS:</from>

<p>Staff provision in the Post Office is directly related to the demand for the various services it renders and although a decrease of 1,8&#x0025; is anticipated in the number of personnel at the end of the financial year which ended on 31 March 1988 as compared to that at the end of the previous financial year, there is no indication at this stage that there will be a drastic decrease in the number of staff members for the 1988/89 financial year. In line with standing practice, the Department will, however, continue to &#x2014;</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) abolish posts that may become redundant; and</li>

<li>(b) hold the filling of vacancies that occur in abeyance if necessary, without detrimentally affecting the rendering of service.</li>

</ol>

<p>It is not the intention to retrench personnel with a view to effecting reductions. Everything possible is being done to place persons who become redundant at a specific office or section, elsewhere where there is a need for their services.</p>

</answer>

<heading>Telephone accounts in arrears</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_communications">

<num>753.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Communications:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) How many telephone subscribers residing in the Black residential areas of South Africa were in arrears with their telephone accounts (a) as at the latest specified date for which information is available and (b) as at the same date in the previous year;</li>

<li>(2) whether such subscribers are allowed a period of grace prior to their telephone services being discontinued; if so, (a) what is this period and (b) what charges are levied for reconnecting such services?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_communications" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF COMMUNICATIONS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) and (b) Since the large majority of the approximately 1 600 exchanges in the country serve both Black and White clients and as these groups are not reflected separately in our records, the compilation of the required information will be a task of considerable magnitude which cannot be justified;</li>

<li>(2) yes,

<ol>

<li>(a) and (b) from the time of the rendering of an account, from 45 to 51 days are allowed before a service is suspended due to non-payment. Should payment be effected after suspension, the service is restored and a reconnection fee of R30,00 levied. If payment is, however, not effected within a reasonable period after suspension, the client is informed in writing that steps are being taken to discontinue the service. Should the service thus be discontinued and the client requires restoration the request is treated as a new application and the full installation charge of R125,00 is payable in addition to any outstanding amounts in respect of his previous service. Depending on <span class="col_1055-1056" refersTo="page_0607"/>circumstances, an advance payment may also be collected to protect the Department&#x2019;s income.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>

<num>915.</num>

<p>asked the Minister of Communications:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether employees in his Department are permitted to (a) take on extra employment and (b) participate in any type of business of their own; if so,</li>

<li>(2) whether this permission is granted subject to any conditions; if so, what conditions?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_communications" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF COMMUNICATIONS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) and (b) Yes; subject to prior permission being obtained in terms of Section 38(1)(b) of the Post Office Service Act, 1974;</li>

<li>(2) yes, on condition that &#x2014;

<ol>
