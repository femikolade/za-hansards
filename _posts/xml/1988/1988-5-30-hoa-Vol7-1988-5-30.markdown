<heading>Savannah Park: development delayed</heading>

<question by="#devan" to="#minister_of_housing">

<num>80.</num>

<from>Mr P I DEVAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Housing:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether the development of Savannah Park Phase 1 has been delayed; if so, (a) why, (b) how long is this delay expected to last, (c) when will development be (i) resumed and (ii) completed and (d) what does this phase comprise;</li>

<li>(2) whether tenders were invited for the development of Savannah Park; if not, why not; if so, (a) when, (b) in what publications, (c) how many tenders were received and (d)(i) who were the successful tenderers and (ii) what was the amount tendered by each;</li>

<li>(3) how many (a) dwellings will be constructed in this area and (b) plots of land will be made available for development by individual applicants?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_housing" to="#devan">

<from>The MINISTER OF HOUSING:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(a) The performance of the contractor appointed to execute the services contract was unsatisfactory and the Department encountered difficulty in having him evicted from the site. The consulting engineers were also replayed as a result of their poor performance. Furthermore, the September 1987 floods caused so much damage that it delayed the preparation of a specification of the work still to be done, before tenders can be invited for the completion of the services contract.</li>

<li>(b) Until August 1988.</li>

<li>(c) (i) August 1988.

<ol>

<li>(ii) February 1989.</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(d) Approximately 495 Special residential sites.<br/>

<p>1 General residential site (&#x00B1; 50 units).</p>

<p>3 Group-housing sites (10 units each).</p>

<p>1 High School site.</p>

<p>2 Primary School sites.</p>

<p>4 Commercial sites.</p>

<p>3 Cr&#x00E8;che sites.</p>

<p>3 Religious sites.</p>

<p>2 Local authority sites.</p>

<p>1 Garage site.</p>

<p>8 Open space sites.</p>

</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(2) Yes, but for services only.

<ol>

<li>(a) September 1983.</li>

<li>(b) State Tender Bulletin.</li>

<li>(c) Nine.</li>

<li>(d) (i) D E Classen (Pty) Ltd.

<ol>

<li>(ii) R3 280 480,93.</li>

</ol>

</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(3) (a) Initially approximately 150 low cost dwellings.

<ol>

<li>(b) No definite decision has been taken in this regard.</li>

</ol>

</li>

</ol>

</answer>
