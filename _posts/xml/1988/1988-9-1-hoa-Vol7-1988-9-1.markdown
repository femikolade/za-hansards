<heading>RSA diplomatic missions: alien staff members</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_foreign_affairs">

<num>1303.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Foreign Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) What is the policy of his Department in regard to appointing aliens to serve as members of the Republic&#x2019;s diplomatic missions abroad;</li>

<li>(2) whether any aliens have been appointed as staff members of such missions; if so, (a) in what capacities do they serve, (b) what percentage of the total staff complement of his Department do they comprise and (c) in respect of what date is this information furnished;</li>

<li>(3) whether any tasks currently performed by such aliens can be performed by South African citizens; if so,</li>

<li>(4) whether he intends replacing the aliens concerned by South African citizens; if not, why not?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_foreign_affairs" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Aliens are not appointed as members of the Republic&#x2019;s diplomatic missions abroad.</li>

<li>(2) It is not in the country&#x2019;s interest to furnish this information publicly.</li>

<li>(3) and (4) fall away.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Johannesburg: Management Committees</heading>

<question by="#george" to="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning">

<num>43.</num>

<from>Mr T R GEORGE</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Constitutional Development and Planning:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether, with reference to the reply of the Minister of Local Government, Housing and Agriculture to Question No 2, standing over, on 15 June 1988, the Johannesburg Coloured Management Committee has the power to take decisions on its own; if so, in terms of what statutory provisions and/or regulations;</li>

<li>(2) whether the Johannesburg White Management Committee has the power to (a) instruct, and (b) arbitrate disputes involving the Coloured Management Committee; if so,(i) why and (ii) in terms of what statutory provisions and/or regulations;</li>

<li>(3) whether any decisions concerning the Coloured population of Johannesburg are taken by the White Management Committee; if not, why not; if so, what decisions;</li>

<li>(4) whther he will make a statement on the matter?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning" to="#george">

<from>The MINISTER OF CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING:</from>

<p>This matter vests in the Adminsitrator of the Transvaal and he furnished the following information:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) The Johannesburg Coloured Management Committee (now known as the South Western Management Committee) has full power to take decisions in regard to any matter

<ol>

<li>(a) delegated to it by the Johannesburg City Council in terms of section 2A of the Local Government (Extension of Powers) Ordinance No 22 of 1962;</li>

<li>(b) with which it may be charged in accordance with, but subject to the provisions of section 17B of the Promotion of Local Government Affairs Act, 91 of 1983;</li>

<li>(c) in respect of which it may be empowered to take a decision in terms of the Regulations promulgated unuder Administrator&#x2019;s Notice 912 of 4 August 1976.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) No.</li>

<li>(b) No. The White Management Committee is not an arbitrator. However, meetings between the White Management Committee and the South Western Management Committee are frequently held to discuss matters of mutual concern.

<ol>

<li>(i) and (ii) Fall away.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

<li>(3) The Johannesburg White Management Committee may take decisions involving the Coloured population of Johannesburg in respect of any matter which, by law, it is empowered to do and;

<ol>

<li>(a) which has not been reserved for decision by the full Council; or</li>

<li>(b) which has not been delegated to the South Western Management Committee for a decision in accordance with the conditions pertaining to such delegation.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(4) No.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
