<heading>KwaNdebele: civil claims against police force</heading>

<question by="#le_roux" to="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning">

<num>1069.</num>

<from>Mr F J LE ROUX</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Constitutional Development and Planning:&#x2020;</p>

<p>Whether any civil claims were brought against the police force of KwaNdebele during the period 1 January 1986 to 31 December 1987; if so, (a) how many such claims were (i) brought and (ii) disposed of, (b) what total amount was involved, (c) what did the legal costs amount to and (d) what total amount has already been granted by the court against this police force?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning" to="#le_roux">

<from>The MINISTER OF CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING:</from>

<p>The police function was transferred to the KwaNdebele Government on 1 April 1986. I am not in a position or prepared to reply to questions regarding matters which fall under the jurisdiction of another government.</p>

<p>(a), (b), (c) and (d) fall away.</p>

</answer>

<heading>Black residential areas: water/electricity accounts</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning">

<num>1169.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Constitutional Development and Planning:</p>

<p>(a) What was the total amount outstanding in respect of unpaid (i) water and (ii) electricity accounts in the Black residential areas situated in the Pretoria/Witwatersrand/Vaal Triangle areas of the Transvaal &#x00E1;s at the latest specified date for which figures are available and (b) for how long, on average, had these amounts been outstanding?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING:</from>

<p>This matter vests in the Administrator of Transvaal and he furnished the following information:</p>

<p>(a) These statistics are unfortunately not kept in detail and therefore only the total outstanding rent and service charges can be furnished.</p>

<p>Total amount outstanding as at 31 March 1988 is: R328 840 294.</p>

<p>The outstanding amount is mainly in respect of Soweto and the West Rand. It can be mentioned, however, that there is a remarkable decline in the increase of arrears.</p>

<p>The following steps are taken to improve the collection of rent and service charges.</p>

<p><b>By Local Authorities:</b></p>

<ol>

<li>(a) A deacon action is being launched by employing teams to collect outstanding moneys from house to house with reasonable success.</li>

<li>(b) In some instances mobile offices are utilised to collect rent and service charges before normal office hours and in the evenings.</li>

<li>(c) Eviction orders are being obtained against non-payers and electricity supply is being discontinued.</li>

<li>(d) Personal interviews are being conducted with lessees.</li>

<li>(e) Council members hold ward meetings with lessees to motivate them to pay.<br/><span class="col_2273-2274" refersTo="page_1206"/><b>By the Provincial Government of Transvaal:</b></li>

<li>(f) Meetings are held between members of the Executive Committee and the Administrator and Mayors and Executive Committee Members of local authorities, to address the issue, i.e. Soweto, Lekoa, Dobsonville, Jouberton and Ratanda.</li>

<li>(g) Councils which do not attempt to bring its financial matters in order, are moved from office and administrators are appointed, i.e. Diepmeadow, Embalenhle and Tokoza.</li>

<li>(h) In the case of Soweto the Dr. Simon Brand Working Group has been appointed to address the financial problems of Soweto. Recommendations of the Working Group will be applied to other local authorities.</li>

</ol>

<p>(b) &#x00B1; 13 months.</p>

</answer>

<heading>Beaches: open/reserved</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning">

<num>1179.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Constitutional Development and Planning:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether there are any beaches on the coastlines of the Cape Province and Natal that are open to members of all race groups; if so, which beaches;</li>

<li>(2) whether any beaches on these coastlines are reserved solely for use by (a) Whites, (b) Coloureds, (c) Indians and (d) Blacks; if so, (i) which beaches in each case, (ii) in terms of what statutory provisions and/or regulations and (iii) what are the penalties for contravening these statutory provisions and/or regulations?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING:</from>

<p>This matter vests in the Administrators of the Cape Province and Natal and they furnished the following information:</p>

<p>NATAL</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes; all beaches are open to all members of all race groups except Durban&#x2019;s South Beach, Addington Beach and Anstey&#x2019;s Beach; and Richards Bay&#x2019;s Alkant Beach.</li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) Whites, yes</li>

<li>(b) Coloureds, no</li>

<li>(c) Indians, no</li>

<li>(d) Blacks, no

<ol>

<li>(i) Durban&#x2019;s South Beach, Addington Beach and Anstey&#x2019;s Beach; and Richards Bay&#x2019;s Alkant Beach.</li>

<li>(ii) Durban<br/>In terms of the Durban Beach Bylaws published in terms of the Local Authorities Ordinance, No 25 of 1974.<br/>Richards Bay<br/>In terms of the Reservation of Separate Amenities Act, No 49 of 1953.</li>

<li>(iii) Durban<br/>As prescribed by the Durban Beach Bylaws made in terms of the Local Authorities Ordinance No 25 of 1974.<br/>Richards Bay<br/>As prescribed by Section 2(2) of the Reservation of Separate Amenities Act, No 49 of 1953.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

<p>CAPE PROVINCE</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes. It is, however, not possible to provide a list of the open beaches. Local authorities were until recently responsible for demarcation resulting in that all information is at present only available from the different local authorities. The Cape Provincial Government is presently obtaining the information.</li>

<li>(2) (a), (b), (c), (d) and (i) Fall away. (See (1) above.)

<ol>

<li>(ii) In accordance with the reservations of Separate Amenities Act, Act No 49 of 1953.</li>

<li>(iii) As prescribed by the Separate Amenities Act, Act No 49 of 1953.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>
