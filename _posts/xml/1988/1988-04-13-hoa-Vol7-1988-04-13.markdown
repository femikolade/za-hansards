<heading>Municipal police in Black townships: identification</heading>

<question by="#suzman" to="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning">

<num>618.</num>

<from>Mrs H SUZMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Constitutional Development and Planning:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether members of municipal police forces in Black townships are required to (a) wear some visible form of identification and (b) produce their identification on demand by any member of the public; if so, what form of identification are they required to (i) wear and (ii) produce on demand; if not, why not;</li>

<li>(2) whether any action is to be taken regarding the wearing and production of identification by these policemen; if not, why not; if so, what action;</li>

<li>(3) whether these policemen are allowed to perform their duties in plain clothes; if so, under what circumstances;</li>

<li>(4) whether any complaints have been received regarding the actions of these municipal policemen; if so, (a) how many, (b) what was the nature of the complaints and (c) what action was taken as a result?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning" to="#suzman">

<from>The MINISTER OF CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING:</from>

<p>The execution provisions of section 34 of the Black Local Authority Act, 1982 (Act No 102 of 1982) in terms of which the municipal police function was assigned to the various provincial governments on 1 October 1986 and according to information received from them, the following replies are furnished:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1)

<ol>

<li>(a) Yes.</li>

<li>(b) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(i) Besides the prescribed uniform, badges are worn by the officers while on duty as prescribed by legislation.</li>

<li><span class="col_949-950" refersTo="page_0554"/>(ii) An appintment certificate issued when appointed; and a document that declares the official as a peace officer.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) As answered in question 1(b)(i) and (ii) wearing and produce of identification.</li>

<li>(3) Yes, only in the Cape Province when the commanding officer deems it necessary while the officials execute their duties as peace officers even when they are not on duty.</li>

<li>(4)

<ol>

<li>(a) No complaints were received concerning the conduct of the municipal police in the Orange Free State and Natal. In Transvaal and the Cape Province 349 complaints were received.</li>

<li>(b) Theft: reckless and negligent driving; unlawful arrest and assault; pointing of firearm; vehicle driven without licence or consent; under the influence of liquor while on duty; pointing of firearm and threatened with death; murder; attempted murder and assault; culpable homicide; serious assault; assault; unlawful arrest; theft of weapon; damage to property and attempted rape.</li>

<li>(c) Each complaint received is investigated by the local authority or the South African Police. Complaints with substance are taken further by the local authority or the South African Police. Upon conviction those concerned are sentenced. Thereafter departmental disciplinary steps are taken in accordance with staff regulations.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>
