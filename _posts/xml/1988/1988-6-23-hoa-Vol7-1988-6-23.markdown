<li>(1) In August 1987 the Government and the Reserve Bank were approached by members of the Muslim Community with a view to acquiring approval for the establishment of an Islamic Bank in South Africa that would conduct its business in accordance with Islamic principles. The Reserve bank agreed in principle to the establishment of such a bank, provided that the requirements of the Banks Act would be fully met.<br/>

<p>On 16 September 1987 a Durban-based group, represented by Mr A B Mohamed, gave notice ot its intention to acquire the Reserve Bank&#x2019;s approval for the establishment of an Islamic Bank. Subsequently, discussions were held with the Reserve Bank and an information document, dated 15 October 1987, was submitted to the Bank, setting out some of the principles on which the proposed banking operation would be based. The promotors were informed that the document did not contain sufficient information to qualify as a formal application for the establishment of a new bank. A further application has not been submitted subsequently.</p>

<p>On 18 September 1987 Mr E E Kharsany on behalf of the Islamic Corporation Limited, an established financial company in Johannesburg, submitted an application to the Reserve bank for permission to convert the Islamic Corporation into an Islamic Bank. Similar applications had been lodged with the former Registrar of Banks before the transfer of the administration of the Banks Act to the Reserve Bank.</p>

</li>

<li>(2) The Reserve Bank has not proceeded with the formal registration of an Islamic Bank. In discussions and correspondence with the two interested parties the Reserve Bank has since September 1987 repeatedly reiterated its point of view that in principle there is no objection to the establishment of an Islamic Bank in South Africa, subject however to the conditions that there should be only one bank that would serve the interests of the entire Muslim Community, and that the proposed bank should comply fully with all requirements of the Banks Act.<br/>

<p>The reason why a banking licence has not yet been issued is that the two promoting groups have failed to reach agreement on a shareholding, a board of directors and a management for the proposed bank that would represent the interests of both groups.</p>

<num>60.</num>

<from>Mr K CHETTY</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Law and Order:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he intends appointing an Indian to the rank of brigadier in the South African Police; if not, why not; if so, when;</li>

<li>(2) whether he will make a statement on the matter?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_law_and_order" to="#chetty">

<from>The MINISTER OF LAW AND ORDER:</from>

<p>(1) and (2)</p>

<p>Appointments to the rank of brigadier only take place on the basis of a thorough evaluation of the needs when vacancies arise and when the essential funds are available for this purpose.</p>

<p>When an officer is considered for appointment to the rank of brigadier, several other factors are also taken into consideration, <i>inter alia</i>;</p>

<ul>

<li>&#x2014; the seniority of the officer concerned;</li>

<li>&#x2014; whether he can be transferred to another centre and is prepared to be transferred to that centre if his duties are required there in the interests of the Force;</li>

<li>&#x2014; whether he has the required minimum qualification;</li>

<li>&#x2014; whether he successfully completed the required management development course;</li>

<li>&#x2014; whether he proved himself to be a versatile administrator who has an outstanding knowledge of all facets of police duties and can maintain good public and personnel relations;</li>

<li>&#x2014; whether he has the psychological ability to cope with the responsibilities attached to such a senior appointment; and</li>

<li>&#x2014; whether he is of good character and his moral life is beyond reproach.</li>

</ul>

<p>Should a vacancy for a brigadier arise and an Indian Officer complies with all the above-mentioned requirements for appointment to this rank, his promotion to the rank of brigadier will be considered.</p>

</answer>
