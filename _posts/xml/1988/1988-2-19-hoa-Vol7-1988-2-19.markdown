<heading>Unemployment Insurance Fund: balance</heading>

<question by="#soal" to="#minister_of_manpower">

<num>20.</num>

<from>Mr P G SOAL</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Manpower:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) What was the balance of the Unemployment Insurance Fund at the end of 1987;</li>

<li>(2) (a) what was the total amount (i) paid into the Fund by State employers and employees and (ii) paid out in benefits in that year and (b) to how many applicants were benefits paid;</li>

<li>(3) (a) what is the present average rate of interest received by the Fund and (b) what amount was paid from the Fund in 1987 in respect of administration costs;</li>

<li>(4) (a) what total amount in unclaimed money is held in the Fund and (b) how many persons are involved in this amount;</li>

<li>(5) how many employers were registered with the Unemployment Insurance Fund as at 31 December 1987?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_manpower" to="#soal">

<from>The MINISTER OF MANPOWER:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) R309 650 152</li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a)

<ol>

<li>(i) R24 977 480</li>

<li>(ii) Separate figures on the total amounts paid in benefits to unemployed contributors in the various sectors, including those who were in the employment of the State, are not kept. The total amount paid out in benefits to all beneficiaries amount to R388 274 527.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(b) A total of 395 888 applicants who were in the employment of the State and the private sector received benefits during 1987. Separate figures on the number of unemployed contributors who were in the employment of the State, who received benefits during 1987, are not kept.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(3)

<ol>

<li>(a) 8,68 per cent.</li>

<li>(b) R23 585 013</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(4)

<ol>

<li>(a) Particulars are not available.</li>

<li>(b) Figures are not available.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(5) 135 567</li>

</ol>

<p>NOTE: All of the above-mentioned figures are provisional and subject to audit.</p>
