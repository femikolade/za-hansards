<p>What was the (a) shortage of teachers and (b) percentage of inadequately trained teachers in Black schools in each specified departmental region in the Republic as at the latest specified date for which figures are available?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_education_and_development_aid" to="#andrew">

<from>The MINISTER OF EDUCATION AND DEVELOPMENT AID:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) No vacancies exist.</li>

<ol>
