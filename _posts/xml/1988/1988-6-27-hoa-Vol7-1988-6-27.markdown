<heading>SAA: foreign nationals as cabin attendants</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_transport_affairs">

<num>1257.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Transport Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether foreign nationals may be employed as cabin attendants on the domestic routes of the South African Airways; if so, (a) since when and (b) in terms of what statutory provisions and/or regulations;</li>

<li>(2) whether any foreign nationals are so employed; if so, (a) how many, (b) what are their countries of origin and (c) in respect of what date is this information furnished?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_transport_affairs" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF TRANSPORT AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) No, not any more. Prior to 1982 it was SA Airways&#x2019; practice to employ foreign nationals on a contractual basis, partly because of a shortage of personnel, but primarily due to the advantage offered by a foreign language.</li>

<li>(2) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(a) Seven.</li>

<li>(b) Portugal, United Kingdom, Italy and the Netherlands.</li>

<li>(c) 9 June 1988.</li>

</ol>

</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<ol>
