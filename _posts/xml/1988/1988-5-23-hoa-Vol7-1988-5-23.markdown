<heading>Eskom: postponed payments of amounts due</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_administration_and_privatisation">

<num>989.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Administration and Privatisation:</p>

<p>With reference to the reply to Question No 752 on 29 March 1988, (a) how many of the 313 large consumers which exceeded the period of grace allowed for payment of amounts due to Eskom in respect of bulk electricity were municipalities, (b) what was the total amount outstanding and (c) in respect of what 12-month period is this information furnished?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_administration_and_privatisation" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF ADMINISTRATION AND PRIVATISATION:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) Of the 313 bulk supply points which were in arrears at one or other time during the 12-month period 1 April 1987 to 31 March 1988, 197 were municipal accounts of which most were in rural areas.</li>

<li>(b) and (c) At the end of January 1988 there was an amount of R5 173 712,48 outstanding in respect of accounts rendered to municipalities for electricity for the month of December 1987. All municipalities are however paying on a regular basis and by the end of March 1988 this amount has been reduced to nil.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Vegetable oil: importing/exporting</heading>

<question by="#van_wyk" to="#minister_of_agriculture">

<num>1014.</num>

<from>Mr W J D VAN WYK</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture:&#x2020;</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether any vegetable oil was imported in the first quarter of 1988; if so, (a) why and (b) how many tons;</li>

<li>(2) whether part of the new season&#x2019;s production of vegetable oil is to be exported; if so, (a) why, (b) how many tons and (c) to which countries;</li>

<li>(3) whether any such oils has been or is to be carried over to the new season; if so, how many tons?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_agriculture" to="#van_wyk">

<from>The MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes;

<ol>

<li>(a) to supply the shortage which resulted from the drought during the 1987/88 season and to meet the demand for special oils which are not available locally;</li>

<li>(b) approximately 30 045 tons;</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(2) yes;

<ol>

<li>(a) as a result of an expected surplus production of groundnuts and sunflower seeds;</li>

<li>(b) it is still too early in the new season to make an estimate of the quantities which could be available for export;</li>

<li>(c) oil expressers will decide themselves to which countries they are going to export;</li>

</ol>

</li>

<li>(3) yes, but the quantities cannot be determined at this stage.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
