<heading>Non-Whites: purchase of time-share units in share-block companies</heading>

<num>802.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Economic Affairs and Technology:</p>

<p>Whether non-Whites may purchase time-share units in share-block companies holding properties in White group areas; if so, (a) in terms of what statutory provisions and (b) under what conditions?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_economic_affairs_and_technology" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF ECONOMIC AFFAIRS AND TECHNOLOGY:</from>

<p>Yes.</p>

<ol>

<heading>Prisoners: bedding</heading>

<question by="#van_eck" to="#minister_of_justice">

<num>856.</num>

<from>Mr J VAN ECK</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Justice:</p>

<p>With reference to his reply to Question No 22 on 1 March 1988, (a) how many of each of the items of bedding provided to prisoners are issued to each prisoner and (b) how thick are the (i) sisal and (ii) felt mats that are provided to certain prisoners?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_justice" to="#van_eck">

<from>The MINISTER OF JUSTICE:</from>

<p>(a) Having regard to the local circumstances at each prison, the following items of bedding are issued to each individual prisoner, under normal circumstances;</p>

<table>

<tr>

<td><p>Bed</p></td>

<td><p>&#x2014; One per prisoner.</p></td>

</tr>

<tr>

<td><p>Mattress</p></td>

<td><p>&#x2014; One per prisoner.</p></td>

</tr>

<tr>

<td><p>Pillows and pillowcases</p></td>

<td><p>&#x2014; One of each per prisoner.</p></td>

</tr>

<tr>

<td><p>Sheets</p></td>

<td><p>&#x2014; Two per prisoner who <span class="col_965-966" refersTo="page_0562"/>sleeps on a bed.</p></td>

</tr>

<tr>

<td><p>Blankets</p></td>

<td><p>&#x2014; Two blankets per prisoner, which can be increased during cold climatic conditions according to need.</p></td>

</tr>

<tr>

<td><p>Bedspreads</p></td>

<td><p>&#x2014; One per female prisoner and normally also one per hospital patient. Male prisoners are usually not issued with bedspreads.</p></td>

</tr>

<tr>

<td><p>Sleeping mats</p></td>

<td><p>&#x2014; Prisoners who do not sleep on beds, sleep on two or more sleeping mats of which at least one must preferably be fabricated from felt due to the softness of the product. </tr>

</table>

<p>(b) (i) and (ii)</p>

<p>The thickness of the sleeping mats is as follows:</p>

<p>Two felt sleeping mats = &#x00B1;24 mm One sisal and one felt sleeping mat = &#x00B1;42 mm</p>

<p>Two sisal sleeping mats = &#x00B1;60 mm</p>

<p>For the information of the honourable member it can also be mentioned that a total of 45 791 beds are in use in South African prisons. The new prisons at Durban and Johannesburg are already equipped with beds and several older prisons are also, as far as possible, equipped with beds. All prison hospitals were, at first priority, equipped with beds.</p>
