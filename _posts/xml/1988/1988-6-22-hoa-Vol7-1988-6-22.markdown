<heading>Aliens employed illegally</heading>

<question by="#andrew" to="#minister_of_justice">

<num>872.</num>

<from>Mr K M ANDREW</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Justice:</p>

<p>With reference to his reply to Question No 26 on 8 March 1988, (a) how many aliens were employed illegally by each of the 32 persons convicted of this offence, (b) from what country did each of these aliens come, (c) how long had each alien been in South Africa and (d) what was the penalty imposed on each of the convicted persons?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_justice" to="#andrew">

<from>The MINISTER OF JUSTICE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) to (c) The information is not readily available in the Department.</li>

<li>(d) In my reply to Question No 26 of 5 March 1988 I indicated that 32 employers were convicted of the offences concerned. After the records had been examined to obtain the information regarding the penalties, it, however, appeared that 30 employers were convicted in this regard. The penalties which were imposed, are as follows:<br/>

<p>1 employer was convicted, cautioned and discharged.</p>

<p>2 employers each paid R150 admission of guilt.</p>

<p>2 employers each paid R250 admission of guilt.</p>

<p>10 employers each paid R300 admission of guilt.</p>

<p>7 employers each paid R600 admission of guilt.</p>

<p>1 employer paid R1 000 admission of guilt.</p>

<p>1 employer paid R1 200 admission of guilt.</p>

<p>1 employer was sentenced to R100 or 50 days&#x2019; imprisonment suspended for 3 years.</p>

<p>1 employer was sentenced to R250 or 75 days&#x2019; imprisonment.</p>

<p>1 employer was sentenced to R1 000 or 6 months&#x2019; imprisonment suspended for 5 years.</p>

<p>1 employer was sentenced to R1 000 or 12 months&#x2019; imprisonment suspended for 5 years.</p>

<p>1 employer was sentenced to R2 000 or 12 months&#x2019; imprisonment of which R1 500 or 9 months&#x2019; imprisonment was suspended for 5 years.</p>

<p>1 employer was sentenced to R2 000 or 2 years&#x2019; imprisonment suspended for 5 years.</p>

</li>

</ol>
