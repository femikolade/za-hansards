<num>*25.</num>

<from>Mr R M BURROWS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Finance:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether, with reference to his reply to Question No 30 on 8 March 1988, his Department is considering the recommendation of the Commission of Inquiry into the Tax Structure of the Republic of South Africa regarding the deductibility of expenditure incurred by taxpayers at school level in the form of donations to certain special educational funds; if not, why not; if so, when is it anticipated that departmental consideration of this matter will be finalized;</li>

<heading>Diplomatic vehicles: number-plate system</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_foreign_affairs">

<num>*2.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Foreign Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) What is the vehicle number-plate system used in the Republic for diplomatic vehicles;</li>

<li>(2) whether the United States of America uses a different number-plate system for diplomatic vehicles; if so, what system is used there;</li>

<li>(3) whether this system applies equally to all countries maintaining diplomatic relations with the United States;</li>

<li>(4) whether diplomatic vehicles used there by members of the South African diplomatic corps are identifiable by the letters and digits on their number-plates; if so,</li>

<li>(5) whether he will make a statement on the matter?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#deputy_minister_of_foreign_affairs" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The DEPUTY MINISTER OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Diplomatic status vehicles are registered by the use of the letters DIP, followed by three numbers, not particularly to any country, followed by the letter D, for example, DIP 555 D.<br/>

<p>Consular status vehicles are registered by the use of the letters FCC, followed by three numbers, not particularly to any country, followed by the letter D, for example, FCC 555 D.</p>

<p>The vehicles of the Secretariat for Multilateral Co-operation in Southern Africa are registered by the use of the letters SEC, followed by three numbers followed by the letter D, for example SEC 555 D.</p>

</li>

<li>(2) Yes, in the United States of America diplomatic status vehicles are registered by the use of the letter D followed by two letters, which are particular to the relevant country, followed by three numbers, denoting, in numerical order, the private and official vehicles of the Mission <span class="col_1703-1704" refersTo="page_0925"/>concerned and of its officials. In the case of administrative status vehicles, the letter S replaces the letter D.</li>

<li>(3) Yes.</li>

<li>(4) Yes.</li>

<li>(5) No.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<question by="#chetty" to="#minister_of_housing">

<num>91.</num>

<from>Mr K CHETTY</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Housing:</p>

<p>(a) What is the policy of his Department in regard to the provision of homes for Indian persons falling in the (i) sub-economic group and (ii) economic group and (b) what steps have been or are being taken in this regard?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_housing" to="#chetty">

<from>The MINISTER OF HOUSING:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) (i) and (ii) My Department follows the following policy in drder to supply adequate housing for both groups:

<ul>

<li>&#x2014; the allocation of Housing Development Funds in the most effective way possible in order to ensure optimum access to people for housing;</li>

<li>&#x2014; a sales campaign to alienate all saleable units to present tenants;</li>

<li>&#x2014; encouragement of the participation of the private sector; and</li>

<li>&#x2014; planning of housing projects to be carried out departmentally.</li>

</ul>

</li>

<li>(b)

<ol>

<li>(i) Funds are being allocated to local authorities in order to enable it to satisfy the existing needs</li>

<li>(ii) The sales campaign has been extended on numerous occasions in order to enable tenants to acquire the properties they occupy.</li>

<li>(iii) Funds are being allocated to utility companies and land is being made available to development companies in order to enable it to participate in the supply of houses.</li>

<li>(iv) Departmental projects are carried out from time to time.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>
