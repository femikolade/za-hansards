<heading>SAP: recording of offences</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_law_and_order">

<num>892.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Law and Order:</p>

<p>Whether the South African Police record offences according to (a) suburbs, (b) towns or cities, (c) municipalities and/or (d) regions; if not, what procedure is followed in this regard?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_law_and_order" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF LAW AND ORDER:</from>

<p>(a) to (d).</p>

<p>No. The Republic is divided into 826 police station areas which do not necessarily correspond with suburban, city, town, municipal and/or regional borders. A police station area may, therefore, include more than one suburb, town, municipality or region. In the same way, one city or municipal area may include more than one police station.</p>

<p>Each police station compiles a monthly statistical report of all crimes reported in that police station area. These reports are processed at the Head Office of the South African Police to form one central crime report for the entire country.</p>

</answer>

</question>

<from>The MINISTER OF LAW AND ORDER:</from>

<p>(1) and (2).</p>
