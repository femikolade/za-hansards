<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_national_education">

<num>531.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of National Education:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether his Department keeps a record of the number of foreign students who attended South African universities in each of the latest specified five years for which figures are available; if not, why not; if so, (a) how many were there in each such year and (b) what were their countries of origin;</li>

<li>(2) whether subsidies were paid in respect of these students by the South African Government; if so, what subsidies?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_national_education" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF NATIONAL EDUCATION:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes, where &#x201C;foreign students&#x201D; mean: non-resident aliens (i.e. persons who are in this country on a temporary basis and who do not have the right to remain indefinitely) and foreigners (i.e. persons who are not South African citizens, and who are not living in this country while studying).

<ol>

<li>(a) 1982: 9 485<br/>1983: 12 053 1984:12 782<br/>1985: 15 007 1986: 16 546</li>

<li>(b) See attached table.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) Yes. The subsidy paid to universities as per the approved subsidy formula.</li>

</ol>
