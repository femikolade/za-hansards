<heading>Post Office employees: overtime</heading>

<question by="#derby-lewis" to="#minister_of_communications">

<num>1422.</num>

<from>Mr C J DERBY-LEWIS</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Communications:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) What categories of Post Office employees are paid for overtime and (b)(i) when and (ii) for what period are they paid for overtime at the rate of (aa) time and a third, (bb) time and a half and (cc) double time;</li>

<li>(2) whether this arrangement is applicable to all Post Office employees; if not, (a) what exceptions are made and (b) what are the reasons for these exceptions?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_communications" to="#derby-lewis">

<from>The MINISTER OF COMMUNICATIONS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1)

<ol>

<li>(a) Officials in receipt of a salary not exceeding R35 070 per annum are normally paid for overtime duty. Officials earning higher salaries are only paid overtime in exceptional cases, and</li>

<li>(b)

<ol>

<li>(i) when called upon to work longer than their prescribed weekly hours of attendance, e.g. during periods of staff shortages, absences and work pressure, and</li>

<li>(ii) (aa), (bb) and (cc) overtime duty is divided into two categories:

<ol>

<li>(A) Ordinary overtime<br/>Overtime duty performed during the hours 06h00 and 20h00 on a day other than a Sunday and certain public holidays.</li>

<li>(B) Special overtime<br/>Overtime duty performed during the hours 20h00 and 06h00 on weekdays and on a Sunday and certain public holidays.<br/>Hourly overtime rates are calculated according to a sliding scale that varies between time plus 30&#x0025; on the lowest salary notch to time plus 4&#x0025; on the highest notch in respect of ordinary overtime and time plus 82&#x0025; to time plus 74&#x0025; in respect of special overtime;</li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) yes;

<ol>

<li>(a) and (b) fall away.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>
