<heading>Unemployment Insurance Fund</heading>

<question by="#soal" to="#minister_of_manpower">

<num>25.</num>

<from>Mr P G SOAL</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Manpower:</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) What was the total amount (i) paid into the Unemployment Insurance Fund in 1987 and (ii) paid out in benefits by the Fund in that year and (b) to how many (i) White, (ii) Coloured, (iii) Indian and (iv) Black applicants were benefits paid?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_manpower" to="#soal">

<from>The MINISTER OF MANPOWER:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a)

<ol>

<li>(i) R583 168 280</li>

<li>(ii) R388 274 527</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(b) The Unemployment Insurance Act, 1966, makes no distinction between the various population groups and consequently the Fund does not have separate figures available.</li>

</ol>

<p>NOTE: The above-mentioned figures are provisional and subject to audit.</p>

</answer>
