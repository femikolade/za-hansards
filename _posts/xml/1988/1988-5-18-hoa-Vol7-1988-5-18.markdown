</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_constitutional_development_and_planning" to="#george">

<from>The MINISTER OF CONSTITUTIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND PLANNING:</from>

<p>The Group Areas Board has investigated a proposal for the extension of the Riverlea Coloured group area. <p>The Administration: House of Representatives has, as a result, officially withdrawn its proposal in respect of this land.</p>
