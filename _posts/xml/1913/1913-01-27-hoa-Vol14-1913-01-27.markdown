<debateSection name="#pensions_committee">

<heading>PENSIONS COMMITTEE</heading>

<p>The MINISTER OF JUSTICE, with leave, amended Notice of Motion No. 3, and moved: That a Select Committee be appointed to which shall be referred all minutes recommending special pensions and all applications for pensions, grants and gratuities not authorised by law; the Committee to have power to take evidence and call for papers, and to consist of ten members.</p>

<speech by="#j_krige">

<from>Mr. <person refersTo="hansard_za">C. J KRIGE</person> (Caledon)</from>

<p>seconded.</p>

<p>Agreed to.</p>

<p>The MINISTER OF JUSTICE then moved: That Messrs. Currey, Clayton, Keyter, General T. Smuts, Messrs. Whitaker, and Runciman be members of the Committee.</p>

</speech>

<speech by="#j_krige">

<from>Mr. <person refersTo="hansard_za">C. J KRIGE</person> (Caledon)</from>

<p>seconded.</p>
