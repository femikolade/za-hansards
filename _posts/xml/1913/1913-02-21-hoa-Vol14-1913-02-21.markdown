If they referred to a later clause, they would find that the dealer was liable to a very considerable fine and imprisonment if he sold more than a reasonable amount; and the buyer was also liable to a penalty. There was also a rather important clause in the Bill, which gave half the fine to the informer, and they might have any amount of trouble and worry because of that reward to the informer. Under the new section, the magistrate, or Justice of the Peace, whoever issued the permit, would decide what constituted a reasonable quantity, so that they would have a great deal of worry obviated. It seemed perfectly clear that if this amendment was carried, section 18 would also have to be amended.</p>
