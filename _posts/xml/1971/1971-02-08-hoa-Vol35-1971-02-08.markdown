<heading>Prohibitions on publications previously prohibited lifted by Publications Control Board, 1970</heading>

<question by="#suzman" to="#minister_of_the_interior">

<num>7.</num>

<from>Mrs. H. SUZMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of the Interior:</p>

<p>Whether during 1970 the Publications Control Board lifted any prohibitions on publications previously prohibited by it; if so, (a) on how many occasions, (b) what were the titles of the publications, (c) on what dates were the prohibitions (i) imposed and (ii) lifted and (d) at whose instance in each case.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_the_interior" to="#suzman">

<from>The MINISTER OF THE INTERIOR:</from>

<p>Yes.</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) On two occasions.</li>

<li>(b) &#x201C;Photography&#x201D;, Vol. 5, No. 4, January, 1970, and &#x2018;&#x201C;Island&#x201D;, by Aldous Huxley.</li>

<li>(c)

<ol>

<li>(i) On 27th February, 1970, and 21st August, 1964, respectively.</li>

<li>(ii) On 20th March, 1970, and 4th September, 1970, respectively.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(d) Central News Agency Ltd., Johannesburg, and the &#x201C;Cape Times&#x201D;, Cape Town, respectively.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>

<heading>Visits abroad by officials of local authorities</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_the_interior">

<num>45.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of the Interior:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he has issued a directive in regard to visits abroad by officials of municipalities or other local authorities; if so, (a) on what date, (b) what local authorities are involved, (c) what are the contents of the directive and (d) for what reasons was it issued;</li>

<li>(2) (a) how many such visits have come to his attention, (b) how many of them were approved unconditionally, (c) how many were approved conditionally, (d) what were the main conditions laid down, (e) how many were rejected and (f) what were the reasons for rejection.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_the_interior" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF THE INTERIOR:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(a) 9th December, 1966, and 10th June, 1969.</li>

<li>(b) All local authorities falling under the jurisdiction of the provincial administrations.</li>

<li>(c) That Government approval must be obtained in respect of proposed official visits abroad by members or officials of local authorities.</li>

<li>(d) To ensure co-ordination and cooperation between the Government, the provincial administrations and local authorities in regard to membership of international bodies, the attendance of international conferences and overseas visits for other official purposes.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) 17.</li>

<li>(b) 16.</li>

<li>(c) None.</li>

<li>(d) Falls away.</li>

<li>(e) 1.</li>

<li>(f) The conference concerned was the &#x201C;International Planned Parenthood Conference&#x201D; held in Santiago during April, 1967, in respect of which it was felt that attendance on a local authority level was not advisable, as family planning is of national importance and as such should be undertaken on a national level.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>
