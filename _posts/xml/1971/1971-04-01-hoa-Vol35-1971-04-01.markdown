<heading><span class="col_661-662" refersTo="page_0430"/>Transport for Coloured pupils</heading>

<question by="#taylor" to="#minister_of_coloured_affairs">

<num>430.</num>

<from>Mrs. C. D. TAYLOR</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Coloured Affairs:</p>

<p>Whether any free or subsidized transport for Coloured pupils is provided by his Department; if so, (a) in which areas, (b) what is the total cost, (c) how many pupils are involved, (d) on what basis is such transport available to pupils and (e) what steps are taken to bring such facilities to the notice of parents and pupils.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_coloured_affairs" to="#taylor">

<from>The MINISTER OF COLOURED AFFAIRS:</from>

<p>Education for Coloured persons in the Republic is a matter falling under the control of the Coloured Persons Representative Council and administered country-wide by its Executive. I have, however, ascertained that the following particulars can be furnished:</p>

<p>Yes. The Administration of Coloured Affairs does not, however, subsidize transport, but provides travelling allowances of up to R20 per pupil per year to indigent pupils residing more than two miles from the nearest suitable school for the use of public transport.</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) Transport schemes financed by the Administration for Coloured Affairs are in operation on the following routes:

<ol>

<li>(i) Ncalu, Clydesdale and Umzimkulu to Ixopo (Natal).</li>

<li>(ii) Bethelsdorp, Windvogel, etc. to South End High School (Port Elizabeth).</li>

<li>(iii) Korsten, Schauderville, etc. to Machui Primary School (Port Elizabeth).</li>

<li>(iv) Simonstown, Noordhoek, etc. to Ocean View High School, Slangkop.</li>

<li>(v) Sparks Estate to Umbilo High School (Durban).</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(b) R50 150 per year.</li>

<li>(c) Approximately 1 744 pupils.</li>

<li>(d)

<ol>

<li>(i) The conveyance of pupils to schools where such pupils reside more than three miles from the nearest suitable school and where no public transport facilities are available.</li>

<li>(ii) Temporary conveyance of pupils to existing schools from new housing schemes where school facilities have not as yet been provided.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(e) Where transport schemes financed by the Administration of Coloured Affairs are instituted, such schemes are made known to parents by principals of schools and inspectors of education. Regional representatives of the Administration and inspectors of education are conversant with such facilities and are able to furnish parents with full information.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
