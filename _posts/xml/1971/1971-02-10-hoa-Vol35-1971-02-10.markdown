<heading>Housing loans available to White and non-White Post Office officials</heading>

<question by="#timoney" to="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs">

<num>118.</num>

<from>Mr. H. M. TIMONEY</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Posts and Telegraphs:</p>

<p>Whether housing loans from the Department are available to postal workers; if so, on what basis are the loans made.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs" to="#timoney">

<from>The MINISTER OF POSTS AND TELEGRAPHS:</from>

<p>No, but a scheme exists which enables White, Coloured and Indian officers with dependants, and who are on the permanent establishment, to obtain 100 per cent housing loans up to a maximum of R15 000 from building societies and similar institutions, and whereby the rate of interest on mortgage loans is subsidized out of the Post Office Fund, basically as follows:</p>

<block name="quote">Salary not higher than R4 200 p.a.:<br/>Effective rate of interest payable by officer, 4 per cent.<br/>Salary higher than R4 200 p.a., but not higher than R10 200 p.a.: Effective rate of interest payable by officer, 5 per cent.<br/>Salary higher than R10 200 p.a.: Effective rate of interest payable by officer, 6 per cent.</block>

</answer>
