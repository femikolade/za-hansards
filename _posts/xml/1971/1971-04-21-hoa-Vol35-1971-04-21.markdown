<writtenStatements>

<heading>Overseas broadcasting service of the S.A.B.C.</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_national_education">

<num>530.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of National Education:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) What (a) is the name and (b) are the qualifications of the person who decides what material containing information or news about South Africa should be broadcast in the overseas broadcasting service;</li>

<li>(2) whether this broadcasting service has since 1st January, 1971, broadcast any material at the request of a Minister or a Department, if so, (a) which Minister or Department and (b) what did the material deal with;</li>

<li>(3) whether the broadcasting service has since the same date requested any Minister or department to make material available to it for special broadcasts; if so, (a) which Minister or Department and (b) what did the material deal with.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_national_education" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF NATIONAL EDUCATION:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) and (b) A team of experienced journalists and programme officials.</li>

<li>(2) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(a) The Department of Immigration.</li>

<li>(b) Announcements in connection with recruiting teams that were to visit Europe to recruit staff for SASOL and the Atlas Aircraft Corporation.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(3) Yes, (a) and (b) the following Departments and subjects:

<ol>

<li>(1) Agricultural Technical Services: Combating locusts.</li>

<li>(2) Transport: Air services and Jan Smuts Airport.</li>

<li>(3) Bantu Education: Statistics in connection with schools, pupils and teachers and the names of Bantu teachers with whom interviews could be held in connection with Bantu education.</li>

<li>(4) Forestry: Indigenous trees.</li>

<li>(5) Posts and Telegraphs: Stamps.</li>

<li>(6) Justice and Bantu Administration and Development: Persons with whom interviews could be held in connection with Bantu law.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>
