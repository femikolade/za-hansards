<heading>Earnings of White employees on S.A. Railways</heading>

<question by="#raw" to="#minister_of_transport">

<num>377.</num>

<from>Mr. W. V. RAW</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Transport:</p>

<p>How many White persons employed on the South African railways earn a basic salary or wage of (a) over R6 000, (b) under R2 400 and (c) under R1 200 per annum.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_transport" to="#raw">

<from>The MINISTER OF TRANSPORT:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) 1 269.</li>

<li>(b) 59 175.</li>

<li>(c) 3 709.</li>

</ol>

<p>The figure reflected in the reply to part (b) of the Question includes that shown under part (c).</p>

</answer>
