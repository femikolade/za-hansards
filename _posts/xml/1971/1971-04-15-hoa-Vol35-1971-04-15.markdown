<heading>Employment of Coloured B.Sc. graduates as chemists</heading>

<question by="#suzman" to="#minister_of_labour">

<num>514.</num>

<from>Mrs. H. SUZMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Labour:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether any food processing company applied to his Department in Cape Town for permission to employ Coloured B.Sc. graduates as chemists in its factory; if so,</li>

<li>(2) whether permission was granted; if not, why not.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_labour" to="#suzman">

<from>The MINISTER OF LABOUR:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes.</li>

<li>(2) Permission has not been refused as it is Government policy that employment opportunities must also be created for Coloureds. This policy is carried out in a manner which will not result in a mixing of races at the same work level or a replacement of Whites. During their interview with the Department&#x2019;s local Divisional Inspector, representatives <span class="col_705-706" refersTo="page_0452"/>of the firm concerned could not reply in the affirmative to questions concerning the availability of Whites and they were requested to obtain the desired information. However, they did not approach the Divisional Inspector again.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Establishment of consumer price group indexes</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_statistics">

<num>520.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Statistics:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether sales tax and increases in sales tax on goods and articles which are taken into consideration in establishing consumer price group indexes are included in the indexes; if not, why not; if so,</li>

<li>(2) whether the full amount is included; if not, (a) what proportion is included and (b) for what reasons.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_statistics" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF STATISTICS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes.</li>

<li>(2) The full amount of the sales tax does not necessarily work down to consumers level in all cases, (a) and (b). Since the sales tax is paid at the factory or on importation cases do occur where the manufacturer or the distribution trade bears part of the sales tax and therefore it is not fully included in the price which the consumer pays. The actual prices paid by the consumer are used for the calculation of the consumer-price-group indexes.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
