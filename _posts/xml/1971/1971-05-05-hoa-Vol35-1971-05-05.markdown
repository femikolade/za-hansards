<heading>Radio and television sets: Scales i.r.o. purchase duties, excise duties, import duties etc.</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_finance">

<num>588.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Finance:</p>

<p><span class="col_841-842" refersTo="page_0520"/>Whether rates have been laid down for purchase duties, excise duties, import duties or other duties and taxes in respect of (a) radio and (b) television sets; if so, what rates; if not, when will such rates be laid down.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_finance" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF FINANCE:</from>

<p>Yes&#x2014;in respect of sales duty and customs duty.</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) Radio sets: Sales duty, 30 per cent <i>ad valorem.</i> Customs duty, see schedule (below).</li>

<li>(b) Television sets: Sales duty, 30 per cent <i>ad valorem.</i> Customs duty, see schedule (below).</li>

</ol>

<p>No rates have been prescribed in respect of excise duty or other duties and taxes on radio and television sets and no decision with regard to prescribing such duties has been taken.</p>

<heading>Urban Bantu councils</heading>

<question by="#hughes" to="#minister_of_bantu_administration_and_development">

<num>591.</num>

<from>Mr. T. G. HUGHES</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Bantu Administration and Development:</p>

<p>What urban Bantu councils have been established in terms of the Urban Bantu Councils Act, 1961.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_bantu_administration_and_development" to="#hughes">

<from>The MINISTER OF BANTU ADMINISTRATION AND DEVELOPMENT:</from>

<p>The following 23 urban Bantu councils have already been established:</p>

<ul>

<li>Cape Province: Grahamstown, Uitenhage.</li>

<li>Transvaal: Alberton. Carletonville, Benoni, Boksburg, Ermelo, Johannesburg, Krugersdorp, Nigel, Ogies, Roodepoort. Standerton, Vereeniging, Witbank.</li>

<li>Natal: Durban.</li>

<li>Orange Free State: Bethlehem, Bloemfontein, Kroonstad, Parys, Odendaalsrus, Virginia, Welkom.</li>

</ul>

</answer>

<li>(2)

</ol></li>

</ol>
