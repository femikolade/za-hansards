<heading>Poster of Michaelangelo&#x2019;s sculpture of David</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_finance">

<num>*6.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Finance:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether a poster showing Michaelangelo&#x2019;s sculpture of David was held back by a customs official during 1970; if so. (a) where, (b) on what date and (c) under what statutory authority;</li>

<li>(2) (a) what were the official designation and the public service grade of the post occupied by the official concerned, (b) what are his educational qualifications, (c) what service had he had in this post and (d) what other qualifications did he have for the post;</li>

<li>(3) whether any instruction or guidance was given to the official in regard to the interpretation to be placed on the statutory requirements; if so, (a) what was the nature of the instruction and guidance and (b) by whom was it given.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_finance" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF FINANCE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(a) Pretoria.</li>

<li>(b) 13th July, 1970.</li>

<li>(c) Section 88 read with sections 87 and 113 (1) (f) of the Customs and Excise Act, 1964.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) Official designation of post:<br/>Controller of Customs and Excise.<br/>Public Service grade of post: Administrative Control Officer.</li>

<li>(b) Matriculation Certificate, and National Commercial Certificate.</li>

<li>(c) 22nd June. 1970 to 24th July, 1970 (as Acting Controller of Customs and Excise).</li>

<li>(d) None, except the normal experience of departmental activities.</li>

</ol></li>

In the case in question three posters comprising a single consignment were referred to the Board, two of which (namely &#x201C;Marie, the girl in the wet dress&#x201D; and &#x201C;More Marie&#x201D;) were declared objectionable, while the poster showing &#x201C;David&#x201D; was declared non-objectionable.</li>

<heading>Business collaboration between Afrikaans publishing firms, Transvaal: Inquiry regarding possible monopolistic conditions</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_economic_affairs">

<num>95.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Economic Affairs:</p>

<p>Whether he will institute an inquiry as to possible monopolistic conditions in view of the announced business collaboration between two Afrikaans publishing firms in the Transvaal; if so, what will be the nature of the inquiry; if not, why not.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_economic_affairs" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF ECONOMIC AFFAIRS:</from>

<p>No. The announced intention of the two Afrikaans publishing companies in the Transvaal that they are going to initiate negotiations with a view to establishing a mutually acceptable form of co-operation in order to achieve the rendering of a better service to their customers and a more economic administration, unambiguously stated that the separate newspapers of the two groups would remain completely independent in respect of their policies and editorial staffs and under the control of their respective boards of directors and that no exchange of shares between the two groups are contemplated.</p>

<p>I am, therefore, satisfied that any form of collaboration which may result from the intended negotiations between the two groups would not create a monopolistic condition which would justify an investigation under the Regulation of Monopolistic Conditions Act, 1955.</p>

</answer>
