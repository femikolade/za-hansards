<heading>Valkenberg Hospital: Minor and major works programme</heading>

<question by="#fisher" to="#minister_of_health">

<num>*5.</num>

<from>Dr. E. L. FISHER</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Health:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether the Valkenberg Hospital has been visited and inspected by (a) him or (b) any of his officials during the past twelve months;</li>

<li>(2) whether any plans have been made for improving (a) the condition of the hospital buildings, (b) the working conditions of the staff and (c) the accommodation provided for patients; if so, (i) what plans and (ii) when will these plans be implemented.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_health" to="#fisher">

<from>The MINISTER OF HEALTH:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) and (b) Yes. I as well as several senior officials of my Department.</li>

<li>* (2) (a), (b) and (c) Yes. Apart from the usual renovation and maintenance services undertaken by the Department of Public Works, various services have, at the request of my Department, been included in the minor works programme of that Department for execution during the present and ensuing financial year. In addition, certain services have also been included in the major works programme. Details are as follows: Minor works, additional to those already completed or included in the maintenance programme, and which appear on the 1972-&#x2019;73 programme:

<ol>

<li>(i) The provision of sleeping facilities for boiler attendant at the Coloured Section.</li>

<li>(ii) The division of Sections 3 and 5 in order to effect a better classification of patients according to age.</li>

<li><span class="col_987-988" refersTo="page_0593"/>(iii) The provision of toilet facilities and showers at recreational amenities.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

<p>Major works. The erection of:</p>

<ol>

<li>(i) A training school for Whites&#x2014; Tender date, 1st January, 1972.</li>

<li>(ii) Two prefabricated buildings for 60 White patients&#x2014;Tender date, 1st April, 1972.</li>

<li>(iii) Prefabricated buildings for 240 Coloured patients&#x2014;Tender date, 1st July, 1971.</li>

<li>(iv) Out-patients&#x2019; Department for Coloureds&#x2014;Tender date, 1st July, 1971.</li>

</ol>

<p>The following services were recently completed at the hospital:</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) Sun shelters at the White Section.</li>

<li>(b) Changing-rooms with facilities at the sports grounds for Whites and non-Whites.</li>

<li>(c) Verandahs of Wards 1 and 4 have been glassed in and equipped with adjustable louvres.</li>

<li>(d) The re-surfacing of roads at the Section for Whites.</li>

</ol>

<p>In addition to the above, the following services are contemplated, but it is not possible at this stage to indicate when they will be commenced with, as this largely depends on the availability of the necessary funds:</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) The provision of an admission block for 50 White patients with facilities for specialist and out-patient services.</li>

<li>(b) The replacement of three condemned wards for Whites.</li>

<li>(c) The conversion, after completion of the services referred to in (a) and (b) above, of the vacated wards into facilities for personnel such as offices for medical officers, library, reading and conference rooms, a cafeteria for personnel as well as additional recreational facilities for patients.</li>

</ol>

<p>* Para (2) <i>et seq</i> laid upon the Table with the leave of the House.</p>

</answer>

<heading>Customs and Sales duty i.r.o. motor-cars imported from Japan</heading>

<question by="#van_hoogstraten" to="#minister_of_finance">

<num>656.</num>

<from>Mr. H. A. VAN HOOGSTRATEN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Finance:</p>

<p>What rate of excise or other duties is levied on sedans being imported from Japan in a fully built up condition.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_finance" to="#van_hoogstraten">

<from>The MINISTER OF FINANCE:</from>

<p>Excise duty: Nil.</p>

<p>Customs duty: The customs duty on assembled motor-cars imported from Japan is based on the value for customs duty purposes and the mass of such cars, the rate of duty being:</p>

<p>45 per cent <i>ad valorem</i> and in addition, in respect of each full R100 in excess of a value for duty purposes of R1 000 for each motor-car, 2 per cent <i>ad valorem</i> and in addition thereto, in respect of each full 45 kg in excess of a mass of 1 135 kg for each motor-car, 1 per cent <i>ad valorem</i> with a maximum of the total duty of 100 per cent <i>ad valorem.</i></p>

<p>Sales duty: In the case of motor-cars with a value for sales duty purposes not exceeding R2 050&#x2014;10 per cent <i>ad valorem.</i></p>

<p>In other cases&#x2014;15 per cent <i>ad valorem.</i></p>

</answer>
