<heading>Work reservation i.r.o. barmen employed in Durban and Pietermaritzburg</heading>

<question by="#oldfield" to="#minister_of_labour">

<num>440.</num>

<from>Mr. G. N. OLDFIELD</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Labour:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether a work reservation has been determined in respect of barmen employed in Durban and Pietermaritzburg areas; if so, on what date;</li>

<li>(2) (a) how many applications for exemption from this determination have been received and (b) how many employment posts were involved;</li>

<li>(3) (a) how many of the applications (i) were granted (ii) were refused and (iii) are still under consideration and (b) how many employees are involved in each case.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_labour" to="#oldfield">

<from>The MINISTER OF LABOUR:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes, with effect from 22nd July, 1963.</li>

<li>(2) (3) (a) (i) and (3) (b) Exemptions are granted for specific periods not exceeding one year at a time. The great majority of applications are for renewal of exemptions previously granted. Altogether 306 applications have been received thus far. At present 42 hotels hold exemption in respect of 91 Indian employees.</li>

<li>(3) (a)

<ol>

<li>(ii) One in respect of 3 employees.</li>

<li>(iii) Nil.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>
