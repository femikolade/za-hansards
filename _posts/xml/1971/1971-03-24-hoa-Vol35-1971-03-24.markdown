<writtenStatements>

<heading>New Main Post Office, Durban</heading>

<question by="#oldfield" to="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs">

<num>457.</num>

<from>Mr. G. N. OLDFIELD</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Posts and Telegraphs:</p>

<p>(a) What progress has been made in regard to the establishment of a new main post office in Durban, (b) what is the estimated cost and (c) when is it hoped that building operations will commence.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs" to="#oldfield">

<from>The MINISTER OF POSTS AND TELEGRAPHS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) The erection of a new main post office building in Durban is being considered, but no decision in this regard has yet been taken.</li>

<li>(b) and (c) fall away.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
