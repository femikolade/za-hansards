<heading><span class="col_711-712" refersTo="page_0455"/>Export of coke and recovery of by-products of coal</heading>

<question by="#webber" to="#minister_of_economic_affairs">

<num>*4.</num>

<from>Mr. W. T. WEBBER</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Economic Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether, in view of negotiations for a contract to supply coke to a foreign country, the Republic has the necessary plant to recover the byproducts of coal;</li>

<li>(2) whether he will make a statement in regard to the matter.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_economic_affairs" to="#webber">

<from>The MINISTER OF ECONOMIC AFFAIRS:</from>

<p>I am not aware of any negotiations of the nature referred to by the hon. member which are at present being conducted or which are being contemplated.</p>

<p>Since the known reserves of coking coal in the country are limited, the export of coking coal, as well as coke, is strictly controlled. Only small and occasional exports of coke are permitted from time to time when the local production during short periods exceeds the local demand.</p>

<p>Bitumen in limited quantities is the only by-product which is recovered in the production of coke, and the installations available for the processing of coking coal into coke are, of course, equipped to extract this by-product.</p>

</answer>
