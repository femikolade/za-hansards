<heading>Tents for servicemen during Republic Festival</heading>

<question by="#van_hoogstraten" to="#minister_of_defence">

<num>*6.</num>

<from>Mr. H. A. VAN HOOGSTRATEN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Defence:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) Which company has received the contract for providing tents for servicemen during the Republic Festival in Cape Town, (b) who are the directors of the company and (c) what is the value of the contract;</li>

<li>(2) whether tenders were called for; if so, (a) when and (b) how many were received;</li>

<li>(3) whether the lowest tender was accepted; if not, why not.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_defence" to="#van_hoogstraten">

<from>The MINISTER OF DEFENCE:</from>

<p>(1) to (3) The tents in which members of the South African Defence Force, and that includes national servicemen, taking part in the Republic Festival are accommodated, come from Defence Force stocks which were manufactured over the last 25 years or longer by at least five different contractors. These tents have also been pitched by the Defence Force itself.</p>

</answer>
