<num>148.</num>

<from>Mr. S. J. M. STEYN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Labour:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) How many (a) industrial council agreements, (b) conciliation board awards and (c) wage determinations apply in border industrial areas;</li>

<li>(2) how many in each category apply specifically in such areas and not in metropolitan areas as well;</li>

<li>(3) (a) how many exemptions have been granted from (i) industrial council agreements and (ii) wage determinations in border industrial areas, (b) what type of industry is involved in each case and (c) what is the nature of the exemptions granted in each case;</li>

<li>(4) whether any conditions were attached to any of these exemptions; if so, what conditions.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_labour" to="#steyn">

<from>The MINISTER OF LABOUR:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1)

<ol>

<li>(a) 49.</li>

<li>(b) Nil.</li>

<li>(c) 30.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) Industrial council agreements: Six. The industrial councils were, however, in existence before the areas concerned became border areas. Conciliation board agreements: Nil. Wage determinations: One.</li>

<li>(3)

<ol>

<li>(a)

<ol>

<li>(i) 2.</li>

<li>(ii) 2.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(b) Industrial council agreements: Textile industry; clothing industry. Wage determination: Clothing industry.</li>

<li>(c) In all instances exemption was granted from the wage provisions, whilst in one instance total exemption was granted.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(4) Yes. Exemptions from wage provisions were granted subject to the observance of rates determined either on the basis of a percentage of the minimum prescribed rates or in accordance with a scale put forward by the applicants or as suggested by the Wage Board. Where applicable, it was also a condition or examption that the wages actually paid it the time of application for exemption must not be reduced.</li>

</ol>
