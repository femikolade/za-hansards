<heading>Infant mortality rate</heading>

<question by="#suzman" to="#minister_of_statistics">

<num>*2.</num>

<from>Mrs. H. SUZMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Statistics:</p>

<p>What is the present estimated infant mortality rate in respect of each race group in the Republic.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_statistics" to="#suzman">

<from>The MINISTER OF STATISTICS:</from>

<p>The 1970 mortality of infants; that is to say the number of deaths of infants under one year per 1 000 live births, is as follows:</p>

<ul>

<li>Whites: 21,1.</li>

<li>Coloureds: 136,2.</li>

<li>Asians: 37,1.</li>

<li>Bantu: Not available because the registration of Bantu life-occurrence (births and deaths) is not complete.</li>

</ul>

</answer>

<heading>Drug abuse amongst Bantu</heading>

<p>The MINISTER OF BANTU ADMINISTRATION AND DEVELOPMENT replied to Question *2, by Mr. G. N. Oldfield.</p>

<question by="#oldfield" to="#minister_of_bantu_administration_and_development">

<heading><span class="col_981-982" refersTo="page_0590"/>Question:</heading>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether his attention has been drawn to the recommendation of the Committee of Inquiry into the Abuse of Drugs in regard to the desirability of instituting a comprehensive inquiry into drug abuse amongst Bantu; if so, what steps are contemplated;</li>

<li>(2) whether the problem of dagga smoking amongst Bantu is to be investigated; if so, when; if not, why not;</li>

<li>(3) whether his Department intends to establish rehabilitation centres in terms of the Abuse of Dependenceproducing Substances and Rehabilitation Centres Act in respect of Bantu; if so, what steps are contemplated; if not, why not.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_bantu_administration_and_development" to="#oldfield">

<heading>Reply:</heading>

<p>(1), (2) and (3) My attention has been drawn to the recommendation of the committee concerned. The matter is being considered, but no inquiry has as yet been instituted, and it is not possible to indicate at this stage whether an investigation will be instituted and, if so, when.</p>

</answer>

</oralStatements>
