<heading>Payment of leave gratuities i.r.o. public servants</heading>

<question by="#murray" to="#minister_of_the_interior">

<num>300.</num>

<from>Mr. L. G. MURRAY</from>

<p>asked the Minister of the Interior:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) What payment of leave gratuities is made to public servants in respect of leave accrued but not taken at the time of retirement;</li>

<li>(2) whether the Government has taken or intends to take steps to ensure full compensation for such leave.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_the_interior" to="#murray">

<from>The MINISTER OF THE INTERIOR:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Generally speaking the cash value of the number of days&#x2019; leave with full pay standing to an officer&#x2019;s credit on his retirement, to a maximum of 184 days, is paid to him. This maximum is reduced by the number of days&#x2019; leave with full pay in excess of 92 days taken by the officer during his last year of service. Provision also exists for other leave gratuities which are payable under specific circumstances, depending on the legal or other provisions applicable to the officer concerned.</li>

<li>(2) Representations which were made in the past for the abolition of the aforementioned limitation could, for sound reasons, not be considered favourably. Statistics in regard to the matter are, however, presently being awaited from departments/administrations in the light of which the matter will be reviewed.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Post Office newspaper &#x201C;Postel&#x201D;: Space allocated i.r.o. two official languages</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs">

<num>331.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Posts and Telegraphs:</p>

<p>(a) What percentage of the space in the monthly <i>Postel</i>, excluding space occupied by advertisements is filled by material in each of the two official languages and (b) on what basis is the allocation made.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF POSTS AND TELEGRAPHS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) No pre-calculated percentage of the space in each issue of the paper is filled with material in a specific official language.</li>

<li>(b) News received from the paper&#x2019;s correspondents&#x2014;who, virtually without exception, are post office officials in different parts of the country&#x2014;is published in the language in which it is received. The Editor, as far as practical, makes equal use of the official languages for reports about news which he himself gathers but also takes into consideration the language medium of the person or group of persons to whom the reports refer.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
