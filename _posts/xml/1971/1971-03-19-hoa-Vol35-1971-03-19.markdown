<heading>Survey i.r.o. aluminium smelter at Richard&#x2019;s Bay</heading>

<question by="#mitchell" to="#minister_of_water_affairs">

<num>*1.</num>

<from>Mr. D. E. MITCHELL</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Water Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether a survey of the processes of the aluminium smelter at Richard&#x2019;s Bay has been carried out to ascertain if the factory will produce physical or gaseous effluents which are noxious or harmful; if so, by whom; if not, why not;</li>

<li>(2) (a) what State departments are concerned with the abatement of noxious or harmful effluents from this factory and (b) what powers to control, limit or forbid processes leading to the production of such effluents are vested in any State authority.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_water_affairs" to="#mitchell">

<from>The MINISTER OF WATER AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes, a survey was carried out at the request of the Department of Water Affairs by the National Institute for Water Research of the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research.</li>

<li><span class="col_543-544" refersTo="page_0371"/>(2)

<ol>

<li>(a) The Department of Water Affairs is concerned with the discharge of waste water from this factory. The necessary action is taken after consultation with an <i>ad hoc</i> Committee on which is represented the following bodies:

<ol>

<li>(1) Department of Water Affairs;</li>

<li>(2) South African Bureau of Standards;</li>

<li>(3) Department of Health;</li>

<li>(4) Natal Parks, Game and Fish Preservation Board;</li>

<li>(5) Town and Regional Planning Board for Natal;</li>

<li>(6) Department of Planning;</li>

<li>(7) Richard&#x2019;s Bay Village Management Board.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(b) Powers vested in the Department of Water Affairs to control the discharge of waste water are contained in section 21 of the Water Act 54 of 1956 as well as Permit 264B issued to the Company in terms of the said section and which lays down further requirements. The control of gaseous effluents which might cause air pollution is vested in the Department of Health in terms of the Air Pollution Act 54 of 1965.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>

<question by="#mitchell" to="#minister">

<from>Mr. D. E. MITCHELL:</from>

<p>Arising out of the Minister&#x2019;s reply, can he tell us whether as a result of that research adequate steps have been taken to prevent pollution?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister" to="#mitchell">

<from>The MINISTER:</from>

<p>Yes.</p>

</answer>

<heading>J. G. Strydom Tower, Hillbrow: Provision of facilities for non-Whites</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_community_development">

<num>*6.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Community Development:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) On what grounds did his Department object to the provision of facilities for non-Whites in the J. G. Strydom Tower in Hillbrow;</li>

<li>(2) whether his Department suggested alternative facilities for non-Whites in the Tower; if so, (a) what were the suggestions and (b) with what result; if not, why not.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_community_development" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) The provision of facilities for non-Whites in the J. G. Strydom Tower would inevitably have resulted in a large concentration of disqualified sections of the population at one specific point in Hillbrow, which is not only the most densely populated White group area in South Africa, but is also inundated with a multitude of social problems.</li>

<li>(2) No. The Department is not prepared to support alternative facilities for non-Whites in the overpopulated White area of Hillbrow.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister">

<from>*Mr. E. G. MALAN:</from>

<p>What does the hon. the Minister mean by the term &#x201C;disqualified persons&#x201D;?</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister" to="#malan">

<from>*The MINISTER:</from>

<p>A &#x201C;disqualified person&#x201D; here is a person who is not allowed to stay in that White group area.</p>

</answer>
