<heading>Amendments i.r.o. Post Office tariffs</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs">

<num>290.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the</p>

<p>Minister of Posts and Telegraphs:</p>

<p>(a) What future amendments of tariffs or other payments by persons making use of post office services have been announced by him, (b) when will each one come into operation and (c) what (i) is the present tariff, (ii) was the tariff on 1st June, 1948, and (iii) was the tariff on 1st January, 1970, in each case.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF POSTS AND TELEGRAPHS:</from>

<p>(a) and (b) General amendments of tariffs have been announced and full particulars thereof will appear in the <i>Government Gazett</i><i>e</i> shortly. Since several hundred different tariffs, some in respect of services which were only established after 1st June, 1948, are involved in the amendments, I am furnishing in reply to section (c) of the hon. member&#x2019;s question, information about amendments of only the most important tariffs. If the hon. member desires further information in respect of a particular tariff, I should be pleased to make it available. With the exception of a number of amendments of tariffs for foreign mail which will only take effect on 1st July, 1971, all the tariff amendments come into operation on 1st April, 1971.</p>

<li>(3) whether any provisions exist to ensure that these conditions are carried out;</li>

<li>(4) whether complaints have been received from the public; if so,</li>

<li>(5) whether the complaints have been investigated; if so, by what means;</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF POSTS AND TELEGRAPHS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) 717.</li>

<li>(2) No.</li>

<li>(3) Falls away.</li>

<li>(4) No.</li>

<li>(5) Falls away.</li>

<li>(6) (a) yes, (b) no and (c) yes; in terms of the Radio Act and the Radio Regulations promulgated under that Act, a radio repairer&#x2019;s licence is not issued to a person who&#x2014;

<li>(i) does not hold a radio listener&#x2019;s licence;</li>

<li>(ii) does not himself possess a radio repairer&#x2019;s certificate issued by the Postmaster General under Radio Regulation II; or</li>
