<heading>WEDNESDAY, 9TH JUNE, 1971</heading>

<p><i>For written reply:</i></p>

<writtenStatements>

<heading>Graduate Indian teachers</heading>

<question by="#suzman" to="#minister_of_indian_affairs">

<num>653.</num>

<from>Mrs. H. SUZMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Indian Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) How many graduate Indian teachers with professional qualifications are in the service of his Department in Natal and the Transvaal, respectively;</li>

<li><span class="col_983-984" refersTo="page_0591"/>(2) how many graduates are enrolled in the Faculty of Education at the University of Durban-Westville.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_indian_affairs" to="#suzman">

<from>The MINISTER OF INDIAN AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Natal: 766.<br/>Transvaal: 70.</li>

<li>(2) 72.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>

<writtenStatements>

<heading>Indian education: Inspectors of schools in Natal and Transvaal</heading>

<question by="#suzman" to="#minister_of_indian_affairs">

<num>654.</num>

<from>Mrs. H. SUZMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Indian Affairs:</p>

<p>(a) How many inspectors of schools are in the service of his Department in Natal and the Transvaal, respectively, and (b) how many of the inspectors in each of these provinces are Indians.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_indian_affairs" to="#suzman">

<from>The MINISTER OF INDIAN AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) Natal: 37.<br/>

Transvaal: 10.</li>

<li>(b) Natal: 10.<br/>

Transvaal: Nil.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>
