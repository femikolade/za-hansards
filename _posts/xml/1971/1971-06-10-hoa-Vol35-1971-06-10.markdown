<heading>THURSDAY, 10TH JUNE, 1971</heading>

<p><i>For written reply:</i></p>

<writtenStatements>

<heading>Bore-holes in Western Transvaal i.r.o. sink-holes</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_mines">

<num>642.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Mines:</p>

<p>(a) How many bore-holes have been sunk since 1st January, 1971, to determine whether dolomite areas in the Western Transvaal are safe as far as sink-holes are concerned and (b) what was the depth of (i) the shallowest and (ii) the deepest bore-hole.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_mines" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF MINES:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) 55 by the State&#x2019;s Technical Committee on Sink-holes and 715 by the mines in the area in question.</li>

<li>(b) (i) 9,144 metres (30 feet). (ii) 213,36 metres (700 feet).</li>

</ol>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>
