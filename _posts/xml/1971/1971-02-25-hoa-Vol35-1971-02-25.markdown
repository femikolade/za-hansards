<heading>Public servants seconded to foreign governments</heading>

<question by="#basson" to="#minister_of_foreign_affairs">

<num>98.</num>

<from>Mr. J. D. DU P. BASSON</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Foreign Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether public servants have been seconded for temporary service to foreign governments outside South Africa; if so, (a) to what governments, (b) how many servants in each case, (c) from which categories of the public service were they drawn, (d) for what periods are they expected to do foreign service and (e) what arrangements are made for such servants in regard to (i) housing and (ii) education for children of schoolgoing age;</li>

<li>(2) whether the Government of the Republic pays part of the salaries, if so, what part;</li>

<li>(3) whether any long term agreements have been made with any countries in this regard; if so, with which countries?</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_foreign_affairs" to="#basson">

<from>The MINISTER OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes;

<ol>

<li>(a) Botswana, Swaziland, Mo&#x00E7;ambique, Malawi and Lesotho.</li>

<li>(b) For various periods during the past three years: Botswana&#x2014;1, Swaziland&#x2014;3, Mo&#x00E7;ambique&#x2014;1, Malawi&#x2014;22, Lesotho&#x2014;26.</li>

<li>(c) professional, administrative and technical;</li>

<li>(d) normally from one to three years but in each particular case, the period is determined in consultation with the government concerned.</li>

<li>(e)

<ol>

<li>(i) housing is generally supplied by the receiving government at a reasonable rental.</li>

<li>(ii) seconded officials make their own arrangements but receive an education allowance for each child at school in the Republic.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) It is arranged with the government concerned in each particular case. In most cases the South African Government pays the difference between the the officer&#x2019;s total salary, i.e. normal salary in the Republic plus allowance and the salary which the receiving state offers in respect of the post.</li>

<li>(3) No. Secondment takes place at the specific request of and in consultation with the other governments.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
