<writtenStatements>

<heading>Investigations conducted by Post Office re system of colour television for S.A.</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs">

<num>622.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Posts and Telegraphs:</p>

<p>(a) What investigations were conducted by the Post Office in regard to the best system for colour television in South Africa, (b) (i) by whom and (ii) in which countries were the investigations conducted and (c) what were the details of the conclusions in regard to the (i) PAL, (ii) SECAM and (iii) NTSC system.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_posts_and_telegraphs" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF POSTS AND TELEGRAPHS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) As part of its standing arrangements to keep abreast of developments in telecommunications technology, the Post Office has since 1948 sent representatives to meetings of the relative committee of the International Telecommunications Union and also to demonstrations of certain systems. The Post Office&#x2019;s studies concern only the suitability of specific systems for the transmission of television over its telecommunications&#x2019; channels. Specific investigations covering the field mentioned in the hon. member&#x2019;s question, have not been conducted by the Post Office.</li>

<li>(b) and (c) fall away.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

</writtenStatements>
