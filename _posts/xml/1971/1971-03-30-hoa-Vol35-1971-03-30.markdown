<heading>National Servicemen: Leave, week-end passes and travelling facilities</heading>

<question by="#raw" to="#minister_of_defence">

<num>*21.</num>

<from>Mr. W. V. RAW</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Defence:</p>

<p>What (a) leave and week-end passes and (b) travelling facilities and concessions are granted to national servicemen <span class="col_631-632" refersTo="page_0415"/>during their initial period of continuous training.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_defence" to="#raw">

<from>The MINISTER OF DEFENCE: (Reply laid upon Table with leave of House):</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) Leave.

<ol>

<li>(i) Vacation Leave.<br/>

Seven days plus travelling time to a maximum of four days. This leave is not granted during the first or the last three months of the national serviceman&#x2019;s period of service.</li>

<li>(ii) Compassionate Leave.<br/>

A maximum of ten days plus travelling time to a maximum of four days. This leave is only granted in cases of death or serious illness of the next-of-kin or near relative by consanguinity or affinity in the first or second degree of national servicemen or for serious domestic problems.</li>

<li>(iii) Special leave with pay.<br/>

For examination purposes: In this case leave is granted for the day of the examination as well as for the day prior to the examination.<br/>

For participation in national and provincial amateur sports competitions: A maximum of fourteen days.<br/>

In both cases travelling time to a maximum of four days may also be granted.</li>

<li>(iv) Recuperative leave.<br/>

Can only be granted by a medical officer for any period which he considers necessary. A maximum of four days&#x2019; travelling time may also be granted.</li>

<li>(v) Leave without pay.<br/>

If circumstances warrant it leave without pay to a maximum of twenty-one days may be granted for examinations or reexaminations for the school leaving certificate. In practice only five days are granted. If a national serviceman has to rewrite more than one subject his national service is postponed until the next intake. A maximum of ten days&#x2019; leave without pay may be granted in all other deserving cases.<br/>

Week-end passes.<br/>

No week-end passes are granted to national servicemen of the S.A. Army, S.A. Air Force and S.A. Navy during their basic training, viz. the first six weeks of their national service. Thereafter week-end passes are granted as follows:</li>

<li>(i) S.A. Army.<br/>

One per month for the rest of their period of service.</li>

<li>(ii) S.A. Air Force.<br/>

Two per month for the rest of their period of service.</li>

<li>(iii) S.A. Navy.<br/>

One per month for the next five or six weeks and thereafter two per month.<br/>

National servicemen of the S.A. Medical Services are at present granted two week-end passes per month throughout their period of service. From 1st April, 1971, only one pass per month will be granted.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(b) Travelling facilities and concessions.

<ol>

<li>(i) For leave.<br/>

National servicemen are entitled to train, bedding and meal tickets and transport between <span class="col_633-634" refersTo="page_0416"/>their bases and the station at Government expense in respect of vacation leave, compassionate leave, special leave for study and re-examinations for the school leaving certificate and for recuperative leave.</li>

<li>(ii) For week-end passes.<br/>

National servicemen are entitled to train tickets at concession rate for week-end journeys. The rates are as follows:<br/>

Single journeys: The usual single fare for half the distance to be travelled plus 20 per cent.<br/>

Return journeys: The usual single fare plus 20 per cent.</li>

</ol></li>

</ol>

</answer>

<question by="#winchester" to="#minister_of_planning">

<num>372.</num>

<from>Mr. L. E. D. WINCHESTER</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Planning:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) (a) On what date did the Torlage beach committee submit its report making recommendations in regard to the zoning of the False Bay beach between Swartklip and Macassar and (b) what were the recommendations;</li>

<li>(2) whether any objections were lodged against these recommendations; if so, by whom;</li>

<li>(3) whether the recommendations were accepted; if not why not;</li>

<li>(4) whether any recommendations have been made to him in regard to the zoning of this stretch of beach; if so, by whom;</li>

<li>(5) how is this beach zoned at present.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_planning" to="#winchester">

<from>The MINISTER OF PLANNING:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1)

<ol>

<li>(a) 6th August, 1965.</li>

<li>(b) The recommendations are of a confidential nature and were not made public.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) Falls away.</li>

<li>(3) The recommendations of the Torlage Committee were made to the then Minister of Planning, and his decision was announced in a Press statement on 6th December, 1965, as a result of which a beach was approved for Coloureds. The Divisional Council of Stellenbosch, by means of notice boards, reserved the following area for the exclusive use of Coloureds:<br/>

From the mouth of the Eerste River in a westerly direction as far as the township of Macassar extends.<br/>

The area from the last-mentioned point up to and including Swartklip was left unreserved.</li>

<li>(4) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(i) Mr. H. J. Swart as convenor of an Action Committee representing the community of Kuils River.</li>

<heading><span class="col_661-662" refersTo="page_0430"/>Transport for Coloured pupils</heading>

<question by="#taylor" to="#minister_of_coloured_affairs">

<num>430.</num>

<from>Mrs. C. D. TAYLOR</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Coloured Affairs:</p>

<p>Whether any free or subsidized transport for Coloured pupils is provided by his Department; if so, (a) in which areas, (b) what is the total cost, (c) how many pupils are involved, (d) on what basis is such transport available to pupils and (e) what steps are taken to bring such facilities to the notice of parents and pupils.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_coloured_affairs" to="#taylor">

<from>The MINISTER OF COLOURED AFFAIRS:</from>

<p>Education for Coloured persons in the Republic is a matter falling under the control of the Coloured Persons Representative Council and administered country-wide by its Executive. I have, however, ascertained that the following particulars can be furnished:</p>

<p>Yes. The Administration of Coloured Affairs does not, however, subsidize transport, but provides travelling allowances of up to R20 per pupil per year to indigent pupils residing more than two miles from the nearest suitable school for the use of public transport.</p>

<ol>

<li>(a) Transport schemes financed by the Administration for Coloured Affairs are in operation on the following routes:

<ol>

<li>(i) Ncalu, Clydesdale and Umzimkulu to Ixopo (Natal).</li>

<li>(ii) Bethelsdorp, Windvogel, etc. to South End High School (Port Elizabeth).</li>

<li>(iii) Korsten, Schauderville, etc. to Machui Primary School (Port Elizabeth).</li>

<li>(iv) Simonstown, Noordhoek, etc. to Ocean View High School, Slangkop.</li>

<li>(v) Sparks Estate to Umbilo High School (Durban).</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(b) R50 150 per year.</li>

<li>(c) Approximately 1 744 pupils.</li>

<li>(d)

<ol>

<li>(i) The conveyance of pupils to schools where such pupils reside more than three miles from the nearest suitable school and where no public transport facilities are available.</li>

<li>(ii) Temporary conveyance of pupils to existing schools from new housing schemes where school facilities have not as yet been provided.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(e) Where transport schemes financed by the Administration of Coloured Affairs are instituted, such schemes are made known to parents by principals of schools and inspectors of education. Regional representatives of the Administration and inspectors of education are conversant with such facilities and are able to furnish parents with full information.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
