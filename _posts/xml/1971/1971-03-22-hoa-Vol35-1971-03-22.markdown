<heading>Double sessions in Coloured schools</heading>

<question by="#hickman" to="#minister_of_coloured_affairs">

<num>382.</num>

<from>Mr. T. HICKMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Coloured Affairs:</p>

<p>(a) In how many (i) sub-standard, (ii) std. I and II and (iii) std. III and IV classes are double sessions operating in schools for Coloured pupils and (b) how many (i) pupils and (ii) teachers are involved in each case.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_coloured_affairs" to="#hickman">

<from>The MINISTER OF COLOURED AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) (i), (ii) and (iii) 1 181.<br/>Particulars in respect of (i), (ii) and (iii) above are not available separately. Double sessions are mainly in operation in the sub-standards and, to a lesser extent, in stds. I and II and, in exceptional instances, in stds. III and IV.</li>

<li>(b) (i) 41 350, (ii) 1 181.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
