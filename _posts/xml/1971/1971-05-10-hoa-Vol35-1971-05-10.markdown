<writtenStatements>

<heading>Promotion of local industry for manufacture of television receiving sets</heading>

<question by="#malan" to="#minister_of_economic_affairs">

<num>598.</num>

<from>Mr. E. G. MALAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Economic Affairs:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether he has taken any steps in regard to the promotion of a local industry for the manufacture of television receiving sets; if so, what steps; if not, why not;</li>

<li>(2) whether any provision has been made for expenditure in this connection during the financial year 1971-&#x2019;72; if so, under what item of his Vote; if not, why not.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_economic_affairs" to="#malan">

<from>The MINISTER OF ECONOMIC AFFAIRS:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Notices will appear in the <i>Government Gazette</i> shortly in which intending manufacturers of picture tubes and television sets are requested to register with the Department of Industries and to complete a questionnaire.</li>

<li>(2) No expenses are foreseen which cannot be covered out of the Department&#x2019;s normal provisions.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
