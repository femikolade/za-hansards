<heading>Wheat and maize: Increases in producer prices; increases in consumer subsidies</heading>

<question by="#suzman" to="#minister_of_agriculture">

<num>*1.</num>

<from>Mrs. H. SUZMAN</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Agriculture:</p>

<p>Whether the increases announced on 14th April, 1971, in the producer prices of wheat and maize will be followed by increases in the consumer subsidies on (a) bread and (b) maize; if so, when; if not, why not.</p>

</question>

<answer by="#deputy_minister_of_agriculture" to="#suzman">

<from>The DEPUTY MINISTER OF AGRICULTURE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(a) There will be no increase for the present season since the increased producer&#x2019;s price will apply to the next season which commences in October when the matter will be considered.</li>

<li>(b) The consumer&#x2019;s subsidy on maize is not increased because the producer&#x2019;s contribution to the Stabilization Fund has been reduced by 5 cents per bag and the consumer&#x2019;s contribution of 8 cents per bag in respect of both white and yellow maize, to the Stabilization Fund, and 4 cents per bag to the Maize Offal Fund in respect of white maize, have been abolished whilst the Maize Board&#x2019;s selling prices for white and yellow maize have been increased by 3 cents and 17 cents per bag, respectively.</li>

</ol>

</answer>

<heading>Computerization of customs clearance documents</heading>

<question by="#raw" to="#minister_of_finance">

<num>*11.</num>

<from>Mr. W. V. RAW</from>

<p>asked the Minister of Finance:</p>

<ol>

<li>(1) Whether computerization of customs clearance documents has been introduced; if so, (a) when, (b) at what cost and (c) what saving or increase in staff was effected thereby;</li>

<li>(2) whether any complaints have been received of delay in processing clearance documents as a result of computerization.</li>

</ol>

</question>

<answer by="#minister_of_finance" to="#raw">

<from>The MINISTER OF FINANCE:</from>

<ol>

<li>(1) Yes.

<ol>

<li>(a) The processing of the statistics in respect of imports and exports has been done on the computer since the installation thereof.<br/>As a result of the imposition of sales duty during 1969 the checking of clearance documents in respect of customs duty by means of the computer, as originally intended, could not be proceeded with.<br/>It was decided that the checking of accounts and clearance documents in respect of sales duty goods should take precedence and certain sales duty statistics are being processed by means of the computer since the 1st April, 1969.<br/>Since November, 1970, the auditing of bills of entry in respect of imported sales duty goods has been done by means of the computer.<br/>The processing of accounts in respect of locally manufactured sales duty goods, resulting in the extension of the computer, is at present being implemented.<br/>As soon as the project in respect of sales duty is fully implemented, the use of the computer for matters regarding customs and excise will be proceeded with.</li>

<li>(b) and (c) As the intended project in respect of sales duty has not yet been completed, the costs thereof and any savings or increases of staff cannot as yet be determined.</li>

</ol></li>

<li>(2) No.</li>

</ol>

</answer>
